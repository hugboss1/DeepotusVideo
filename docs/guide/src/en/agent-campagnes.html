You will be able to hand a campaign to the Campaigns agent: it turns a brief into a strategy and a schedule, writes the copy, then puts the posts as drafts in the Scheduler. You approve at two gates and nothing goes out without you. Ten minutes; paid ≈ $, each message shows its cap before you send it.

Before you start

The Campaigns agent works with Claude only: you need an Anthropic key in the vault (see Connect your keys and AI engines). Without a key, it says so on the first send; nothing is greyed out. Its spending is counted under the "campagnes" category; the global cap, the per-engine caps and the space cap apply: see Keep spending under control.

A campaign belongs to one brand space (see Work for several brands): its persona, its forbidden words, its renders. It is not necessarily the space active on screen.

The circuit in short

The brief becomes a folder, then a strategy. Gate 1: you approve the strategy and the schedule. Then come the copy, the images or videos on a quote, and the drafts in the Scheduler. Gate 2: you review and schedule each draft yourself. The agent's circuit stops at the drafts.

Open the agent

  1. Press F1: the drawer opens. It has two tabs, Ask (the quick help, see Ask Deepotus) and Campaigns. Click Campaigns.
  2. Pick the campaign space in the list at the top. Left on No space, the agent only gives directions, and asks you for the space with a card if a step needs a space.
  3. The header shows the space in full and the conversation's total cost. Once the campaign has started, its space no longer changes.

Send the brief: a quote for each message

  1. Describe the campaign in a few lines: the product, the goal, the channels, the period.
  2. Under the input, the cap for this reply appears, for example "≤ $0.13". It is the most the agent can spend to answer, all its turns included.
  3. Click Send: sending means you agree. If you click before the cap shows, the click only displays it; click again to accept it.
  4. The agent reads the space, looks for what already exists, writes the folder, then comes back with the "Gate 1" card: the strategy in a few lines and the day-by-day schedule.

Pass gate 1, get the drafts

  1. Read the strategy. Approve accepts it; Edit declines it and lets you write your correction in the input.
  2. Once approved, send a message ("go ahead"): it has its cap, like every message. The agent writes the copy.
  3. For an image, a video or a voice, the agent posts a quote card with its price: nothing is generated before you click Generate. Decline drops it.
  4. The posts land as drafts in the Scheduler, in the campaign space. The Open the Scheduler calendar card takes you there; if that space is not the active one, it first offers to activate it, on your click.

From the Scheduler

  1. In the Scheduler, click Generate plan. At the top of the dialog, switch from Quick (the one-shot plan, see Plan and publish your week) to Campaigns agent.
  2. Pick the space, write the brief, the start date and the number of days. Attach a document adds a .md, .txt, .docx or .pdf file for the agent to read.
  3. Open the conversation: the brief is placed in the input, with its cap. You send it; gate 1 happens in the dialog, and the drafts show up in the calendar.
  4. Gate 2 is the Scheduler itself: review each draft, fix it, then schedule it or validate the week yourself.

Go further

Continue after a stop

When the agent reaches the cap or its number of turns, it stops dead and posts a "Continue" card with a new cap. Nothing restarts without your click.

After a render

When a video or an image is done, the card switches to Render finished. The agent never resumes on its own: Continue the campaign puts the follow-up in the input with its cap; nothing goes out before Send.

From "Ask"

Ask for a campaign in the Ask tab: the answer carries Open the Campaigns agent, which takes your question to the next tab.

Resume later

During the session, the drawer keeps the current conversation. In the plan dialog, Resume the conversation reopens the latest campaign of the chosen space, during the session too.

From the phone

The paired phone runs the same conversations over Wi-Fi. The work happens on the PC, with its keys and its cap.

The campaign folder

Folder, strategy, copy and review are stored in the app's data, under the campaign space, with what each campaign has learned.

Good to know

Each message shows its cap before you send it; the agent stops dead if it reaches it. These turns are counted under the "campagnes" category (statement in Settings › Pricing & budget); the global cap, the per-engine caps and the space cap apply. Images, videos and voices have their own quote, paid only when you click.

The agent never publishes or schedules a post: everything lands as a draft. It does not change the active space, touch the keys, the Settings or the accounts, and deletes nothing. Ask it to: it shows you where to do it yourself.

A campaign counts its spending and stores its renders in its own space, even if another space is active on screen.
