You will learn to run the growth loop: spot what works in your niche, write a short script, score a render before publishing it, measure your posts, then recycle a winner. The Growth screen is free; each reading or writing paid ≈ $ shows its quote and waits for your click.

Before you start

Connect at least one channel: Instagram for views and comments (see Link an Instagram account), X if you post there. The niche is read from the followed feeds of News (see Follow the news and turn it into posts): add the accounts you want to watch. Everything is stored in the brand space chosen at the top of the screen (see Work for several brands).

Open the Growth screen

  1. Press F1 and open the Growth tab.
  2. Choose the period and the space, then Read now: the metrics of the latest posts of each connected channel are read again, for free.
  3. The Your measured posts, Consistency, Winners and Top posts blocks fill in. The “×” sign compares a post with the median of the ten previous ones of the same channel and format, at the same age: 3× or more makes a winner.

1. Spot the niche's winners

  1. In Winners in my niche, click Choose the niche's feeds, tick the followed feeds that matter, then Keep this niche. Nothing is read again: the screen reads what the feeds have already collected.
  2. Each post shown reaches at least 3× its creator's usual over 7 days. View the original opens it on the platform; Save the winner keeps its metadata, never the media.
  3. Why does it work? opens the analysis: paste the transcript if you have it, click Estimate the analysis, then “Analyse for $…”. Claude fills a fixed grid: hook, named enemy, opening number, promise, call to action.

2. Write the short script

Ask the Campaigns agent (see Run a campaign with the agent), for example “write a short script from this winner”. It stores the script in the campaign and checks it at no cost: hook within the first 3 seconds, one idea per line, ending on a call, same words on screen, the space's forbidden words. To start from a specific video, see Start from a reference video.

3. Score the render before publishing

Ask the agent “score this render”: the score out of 100 is always an estimate. The first stage is free and computed on the video: voice start, longest still shot, pace, subtitles, 9:16 format, length, safe zone. Each remark is time-stamped. An opinion from Claude on four key frames comes on a quote, through a card to accept.

The score follows the post: it shows in the Instagram format panel of the Scheduler inspector and in the approval request sent to the client on WhatsApp.

4. Measure, X included

  1. Each post's views are read at D1, D3, D7 and D30. A post that is too young or lacks hindsight says so instead of showing a misleading number.
  2. For X, whose API is pay-per-use, My X posts shows the checked prices and the quote for the posts to read; “Read for …” starts the reading after your click.
  3. For a daily reading, tick Read X every day, at my cost, under the cap, enter the Monthly X cap ($), then Save the X consent. Without a cap, the consent is refused.

5. Reply to keyword comments

  1. In Keyword comments, set the post's word (“comment GUIDE”) then Save the keyword, and Read the comments.
  2. Make drafts: Free drafts (template) or Drafts by Claude (quoted).
  3. Reread each text, Approve this text, then Send this public reply after confirming. One reply at a time, public only: no private message exists in the application.

6. Recycle a winner

  1. Under a winning post, click Recycle this winner: cards suggest a carousel, three new hooks, an X thread, a “branch” variant and an X video post, each with its quote.
  2. On each card you want, “Accept and write”: Claude writes that single piece.
  3. Create the drafts in the Scheduler stores them 7 days apart, to approve before anything is sent. The “branch” variant is then cut in Edit.

7. Get notified on WhatsApp

  1. In WhatsApp summary and alert, enter Your number and tick Weekly summary, "Winner" alert or both.
  2. Read my templates lists the approved utility templates of the WhatsApp account connected to the space; pick one.
  3. See the quote gives the price of one message and the monthly maximum. Tick I accept this cost per message, then Give my consent. Withdraw approval stops everything.

Going further

Learnings

The Campaigns agent keeps a learnings file per space: formats, hours and hooks above the usual. It uses it for the next campaign.

Reel styles

Nine named styles describe layout, voice, music and pace, with a free quote. They are read through the MCP server; no screen shows them yet.

On the phone

The agent's growth loop is open to the companion: winners, metrics, score and recycling, with the same quote cards.

Good to know

Analyses, writings and X readings are counted under the “campagnes” and “x” categories; WhatsApp messages under “whatsapp”. See Keep spending under control.

Nothing publishes by itself: a recycling gives drafts, a reply is approved and sent one by one, a WhatsApp summary waits for your consent.
