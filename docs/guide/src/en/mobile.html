You will link your phone to the PC, carry your posts and chapters in your pocket, and cut off a lost phone. Fifteen minutes; you need the Deepotus companion app on the phone and the same Wi-Fi.

Before you start

By default, the app only listens on this PC: no other device can reach it. To open it to your home Wi-Fi, replace HOST=127.0.0.1 with HOST=0.0.0.0 in the .env file of the data folder, then restart the app. Windows will ask to allow Python through the firewall.

Pair the phone

  1. Open Settings › Devices. The words this PC only or local network open tell whether the phone will be able to reach you.
  2. Click Pair a device: a QR code appears, valid for five minutes, for a single device.
  3. In the phone app, open Pair and scan the QR code. It closes on its own once read.
  4. To give your keys to the phone, export the encrypted archive from the Vault and open it in the phone app, with its password.

Five devices at most. A paired phone reads what the PC produces; it never sees your keys. It only writes to the PC to drop images and for Avatar live, whose spending goes through your caps.

What the phone does

Sync

The phone fetches the Library and its pictures, and resumes an interrupted download.

Send pictures to the PC

Your photos land in the Library with the “mobile” source, without duplicates.

The week's batch

Take the approved posts and publish them on Telegram or X from the phone. A post taken by the phone is no longer published by the PC.

Inbox

What you share to Deepotus lands here; pictures go on to the PC.

Offline chapters

Take a chapter, write without a network, hand it back to the PC.

Generate pictures

The cost is shown before each generation, at your PC's rates, and counted in your caps.

Avatar live

Film a take and start a Recast, or open a Live session: the PC does the work, the price is shown first (see Play an avatar).

A chapter taken to the phone

While a chapter is on the phone, it is read-only on the PC, with a banner. You can force it back to the PC. If the two versions disagree, the phone's text stays in the log, to copy or take back: nothing is lost.

Remove a device

  1. In Settings › Devices, click Revoke on the phone's row and confirm.
  2. Access is cut right away; the posts and chapters it had taken come back to the PC.

Good to know

Lost your phone? Revoking is not enough: your keys travelled in the archive. Regenerate them with each provider; the Devices page gives the link to each console.

The phone is notified when a render finishes or fails, when a post is published or awaits an action, and when a cap is near.
