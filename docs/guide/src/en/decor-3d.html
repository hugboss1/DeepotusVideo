You will be able to grow trees, shrubs and ground cover at their real size, animate them in the wind and export them, then prepare a generated object as a scenery prop. The whole screen is free: nothing goes to a provider.

Grow a tree

  1. In the 3D Studio, open the Scenery screen, Vegetation panel. No open model is needed.
  2. Pick a species under Trees or Shrubs & ground: 23 in all, each at its real adult height, in metres.
  3. Set the size, the number of branch levels and the foliage: Real leaves, or Cards, lighter for a game.
  4. Pick the Season: in winter, deciduous trees lose their leaves and evergreens get frosted. Bark and tints are set just below.
  5. New variation draws another Seed: another individual of the same species. The same seed and settings always give back the same tree.

The sheet under the view gives height, spread, triangles and leaves, updated on every setting.

The wind

Tick Wind, then set the strength, speed, direction and leaf flutter: the preview moves at once. The wind lives in the preview and in the recipe; the GLB itself stays still until the wind runtime animates it.

Export the tree

Save as a 3D model

Save as a 3D model makes it a 3D Studio model and opens it: Prepare and Export take it from there.

The GLB

Download the GLB gives the tree at real scale, tints included, exported in the browser.

The recipe

Download the recipe (.decor.json) keeps the species, the seed and every setting. Import a recipe rebuilds the same tree later.

The wind runtime

Download the wind runtime (three.js) gives a script that sways the GLB's leaves according to the recipe, in your web project.

Prepare a prop

  1. Open a model (a rock, crate or plant generated in Create), then the Prop panel of the Scenery screen.
  2. Enter the Real height (m), then Set on the ground · new version: the model is centred, set at height zero and scaled, in a new version.
  3. Pick its role in the scenery, from foreground to repeated crowd: the screen shows the advised triangle budget against the model's count.
  4. Triangle budget: Prepare opens Prepare to lighten it; Export the prop opens Export.

Good to know

A species starts from the closest vegetation preset: it is an approximation, not a botanical survey. Adjust shape and foliage by eye.

Prepare opens without preselecting the role's preset: pick it by hand, from the budget shown in Scenery.
