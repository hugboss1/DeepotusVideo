You will export your edit in the right format, at the right loudness, and send it to the Scheduler or to another program. Free: rendering happens on your PC.

Render the video

  1. Click Render in the top bar: the render panel opens.
  2. Pick the preset: master 1080, web 4K, social 720, ProRes 422, H.265, WebM, audio only (AAC, MP3, WAV) or GIF 480. The project's format is kept.
  3. Set the frame rate (24, 25, 30 or 60 fps) and the loudness: −14 LUFS for YouTube and TikTok, −16 for a podcast, −23 for broadcast. A badge compares the last measurement with the target.
  4. Render starts the render; Add to queue puts it behind the others. To export only the I/O range, tick the box provided.
  5. When it is done, a banner offers to send the video to the Scheduler, with your channels, a date and a prefilled caption. Nothing is published without you.

The Delivery view

The bottom bar › Delivery lists the project's renders and previews, their status (queued, running, done, failed), and leads to the Library. Publish sends the last final render back to the Scheduler.

Your own presets

Save this setting… keeps your output setting as a preset of your own, listed with the others.

To another program

› Project › Export EDL… or Export FCPXML… to take the timeline into DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut.

True to the timeline

The render lasts exactly the timeline, frame for frame; a fade is centred on the cut with the source's real frames.

Good to know

Run a 480p Preview first: it is free and shows transitions, effects and mix.
