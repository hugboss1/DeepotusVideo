You will plug in Telegram, X, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok so the Scheduler can publish on its own. Free: all it takes is the access each network provides, stored on your PC.

Where it is set

Open Settings › Connected accounts. One block per network shows how many keys are set and its live status: auto (the Scheduler publishes alone), assisted (it preps the post and you click Send) or not connected.

  1. Expand the network's block and read its note: it says where to get the access.
  2. Paste each value and click Save next to it. The adapter is active right away, no restart.
  3. Check with Verify credentials (or Send test message for Telegram).

Network by network

Telegram

The simplest, in five minutes. Create a bot with @BotFather, make it an administrator of the channel, paste the bot token and the channel chat ID.

X

The four keys from developer.x.com (API key and secret, access token and secret). The free tier allows posting, up to 500 posts a month.

YouTube

Create a Google OAuth client of type "Desktop app", paste its ID and secret, then Connect. Until the Google project passes the audit, YouTube keeps Shorts private, and the Scheduler says so.

Instagram

A business account and a Page token (EAA…) or Instagram token (IG…). Reels are uploaded from your PC, no hosting, up to 100 posts per rolling 24 h.

TikTok

A TikTok for Developers app (Login Kit, "Desktop" platform): paste the client key and secret, then Connect. Without an audit, uploads are private and the account must be too; set TIKTOK_AUDITED to true after the audit.

Connect YouTube and TikTok without copying a code

Connect opens the network's consent page in a new tab: accept, come back, the status turns to connected and the refresh token is saved on its own. The same gesture exists in the Scheduler: the Accounts button, then Connect YouTube or Connect TikTok. That panel also shows each network's quota for the month.

Good to know

A network that is not connected blocks nothing: its posts switch to assisted mode and you send them in one click, or from the phone.
