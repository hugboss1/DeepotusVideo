You will compose a video piece by piece by linking nodes, see it before paying, never pay twice for what is done, reuse a graph like a recipe and have an AI choreograph your texts. Paid depending on the nodes; the graph's cost is shown before running.

The Studio screen

On the left, the nodes palette (/ key) and the starter graphs. In the centre, the graph. On the right, the inspector of the selected node, which can be folded. At the top, the graph bar: New, Save, Recipe, Import, the format, Preview, the estimated cost and Run.

Move around the graph

Drag the background with the middle mouse button to move the view. The wheel zooms around the pointer; the zoom buttons, bottom left, zoom around the centre of the view, from 30% to 150%. The graph always starts below the bar, even when it wraps onto two lines.

The shortcuts box, top right, folds into a badge when a node goes under it: hover it to read it again. Each link lands in the middle of its port's dot; a port name that is too long is shortened, hover it to read it in full.

Compose a graph

  1. Start from a starter graph (Seedance solo, Avatar post, UGC + animation, News reel post, Timeline montage) or from a blank page with New.
  2. Drag nodes from the palette: sources (picture, text, your video), generators (Seedance, HeyGen avatar, pictures), audio, editing, composition, output.
  3. Link an output, on the right of a node, to an input, on the left of another. Click a node to set it in the inspector.
  4. Preview computes the final composition for free: clips not generated yet are replaced by their picture.
  5. Read the estimated cost, then Run. That amount is sent as a cap: the render cannot cost more. Save keeps the graph under a name.

A first and a last frame

  1. Wire an Image node that has a file to the Seedance node's end input: that image becomes the shot's last frame, while the image input stays the first.
  2. In the inspector, models that accept a last frame move to the top of the list; the "Slot last frame" line confirms which file is used.
  3. If the chosen model can't take a last frame, a banner warns that the launch would be refused. Its Veo 3.1 Fast (first and last frame) button switches to that model; nothing changes without this click.

The last frame also goes into a Concatenate, a UGC or spatial composition, and from Quick › Composition. Models that accept it: Seedance, Kling v3 Pro and Standard, Veo 3.1 via fal, MiniMax H3, Wan 3.0, LTX 2.5.

Never pay twice for what is done

The paid render of a Seedance or HeyGen node is pinned automatically: as long as its request does not change, it is reused for free, and the estimated cost says which nodes are.

The last eight renders

The inspector lists the node's last renders; Use picks the one that feeds the rest.

Regenerate

For a new paid take despite the pin, explicitly ask for the node to be regenerated.

Reuse a graph as a recipe

  1. Recipe freezes the graph: its pictures and texts become blanks to fill in.
  2. Run the recipe with new values: a quote is shown, you confirm. Unchanged nodes are reused for free.
  3. From a picture in the Library, Send to also offers to run a recipe on it.

Animate with AI

A text engine can place the words of an Animation for you: where they come in, when, and with which move. Free with a local engine (Ollama); on a paid key, the cost is estimated before sending.

  1. Drag in a Motion direction node (Animations category) and link its output to an Animation's direction input. Link a Text node to its script input: one line per beat.
  2. In the Motion direction's inspector, choose the Style (one built-in sheet per theme), the Source and the Engine; the node's panel sums them up in one line.
  3. Select the Animation, then Choreograph. The elements land in the list; if there were some already, the app asks before replacing them, and Ctrl+Z brings them back.
  4. Preview shows the animation, for free. Without an engine, the built-in choreography splits the script: nothing is sent.

The Customise section adjusts the brief without asking you anything during the call: the sheet's fields (an empty field keeps its default), three sliders — Creativity, Pace, Polish — whose added sentence is shown, a tempo in BPM, the format, the duration, the font and the colours. Next to the model, Effort offers Auto and then only the levels this model accepts.

  1. Import a prompt: title, pasted text, URL of the original page (required), author, themes. The attribution stays on show; the preview shows your specifics added after the prompt, which stays intact.
  2. Install a skill: type owner/repo, then Read the licence. The licence text is shown BEFORE anything is installed; a repository without an MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD or ISC licence is refused.
  3. My library: Filter by theme, Use to make it the node's Source, Delete after confirming. A skill's instructions are edited in the node; Back to the original restores them.

Prompts still belong to their creators. Only import what you are allowed to use: the page URL and the author travel with the prompt, and nothing is fetched from a prompt site on your behalf.

Go further

Engine duel

On a picture generation node, the same prompt goes to two models once you agree on the cost; you compare side by side and keep the winner.

Effects and masks

The Effects / Mask node, plugged into the Render's fx input: grades, VHS, colorisation…, on the whole render or a single layer.

AI fields

A field with a coloured edge is sent to an AI. Its badge shows the model and its price; its microphone dictates your text.

Voice and mix

The Voiceover node picks Voicebox or ElevenLabs; Audio mix dips the music under the voice (light, medium, strong).

Brand strip

Link the Brand strip to the Spatial compose's brand input: its text replaces the template strip's text, the logo stays. The inspector preview shows that text.

Import a graph

Import opens a JSON graph; missing sources are listed and nothing is saved without your consent.

Frame by frame

The result drawer steps frame by frame with the , and . keys.

Good to know

A video generation above the announced cost or your per-request cap is refused before any call. The generated length is capped in Settings; the rest is extended on your PC.

New Seedance nodes start with Seedance 2.5; a saved graph keeps its model. The default model is changed in Settings › Provider defaults.

If the browser cannot dictate, the recording can be transcribed by the server: it is paid, a quote is shown and “No” is the default choice.
