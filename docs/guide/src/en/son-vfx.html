You will generate music, sound effects and a voiceover you direct, store and find your sounds, and add free visual effects. Each generator's price is shown; visual effects and post-processing are free.

The Sound & VFX screen

Open Sound & VFX in the rail. At the top, the generators and their price: music, sound effects (606 bundled sounds, plus generation from a description), voiceover, particles and post-processing. Below, three tabs: Audio, Particle VFX and Post-processing.

Direct a voiceover

  1. In Directed voice-over, write the text.
  2. Click emotion or acting tags (four at most): they go at the start and the Eleven v3 model performs them. The screen shows exactly what is sent to the model.
  3. Generate voice. Trim, add a fade, dip the music under the voice or normalise, then Send to Montage.

Outside the v3 model, or with Voicebox, the tags are removed and you are told.

Music and sound effects

Music

Instrumental or sung: describe the style, write the lyrics section by section (a skeleton is suggested from the persona). Billed per second, under your caps.

Sound effects

Describe the sound and its length: two variations to listen to, which join the Library and the editor's Sounds drawer.

Voice and music mix

Mixes a voice and a piece of music, dipping the music under the voice; the mix lands in the Library.

A character's voice

In the Workshop, a character gets a cloned voice (paid) and a temperament, performed on each of its lines.

Sound effects: pick a model

The Generate SFX card of the SFX rail, like the Generate tab of the Montage's Sounds drawer, has a Model list: ElevenLabs direct or via fal, Sonilo, Mirelo, Stable Audio, each with its price per second and its maximum length. Describe the sound, set the length (0 lets the model choose), then Generate: two variants go to the Library. A model via fal runs in two clicks, the first shows the price.

Add sound to a video

  1. In the Montage, select a video clip on the timeline, then open the Sounds drawer and its Generate tab.
  2. The From the video block names the clip. Pick the model: Sonilo (with "Keep the voice"), Mirelo, Kling, Hunyuan Foley or ThinkSound; some ask for a description of the sound.
  3. A first click gives the estimate, the second runs it. The sound effect goes to the Library; if the model returns the video with sound, it goes there too, under Renders.

AI VFX: relight, remove, reframe…

  1. In the AI VFX rail, pick the source video among your finished renders.
  2. Pick an action: relight, insert a scene, remove a person or an object, day to night, water, colorize, edit with a sentence, change the format, upscale, or a ready-made effect from an image. Each action states its price and what it needs.
  3. Estimate, then run: the first click shows the server estimate, the second, Run for real, runs it. The rendered video goes to the Library and can be placed in the Montage.

The same actions, except the effect from an image, are in the Montage's effects rack, AI section.

The Sounds drawer

All your sounds live in the Sounds drawer: in the Library (Audio tab) and in the editor (B key). Filter by kind (effects, voices, music, imported), between your sounds and the bundled catalog, search by name or tag.

Enhance

Equaliser, noise reduction, compressor and normalised loudness in one click, on your PC, no provider.

Split into stems

Splits music into vocals, drums, bass… (paid, quote on first click); each stem stays linked to its parent.

Isolate the voice

Removes the background from a voice take (paid, quote on first click).

Search by description

Describe what you want to hear; sounds similar to a sound can be listed too. Needs the local search service, otherwise the button says why it is greyed out.

Preview

Hover a sound to hear it (option to turn on); Space plays the selected row.

Free visual effects

  1. Particle VFX: explosions, smoke, sparks, magic… simulated on your PC, to place on a shot.
  2. Post-processing: 16 mm grain, glow, glitch, aberration; applied at render time by the Studio's Render node.
  3. In the editor's effects rack, an effect can go behind the shot's cut-out subject, or in front of it. Cutting out the subject is paid; a first click gives the quote.

Good to know

Split, isolate, cut out: a first click arms the action and shows the price, a second one starts it, Esc disarms. Nothing goes out without that second click.
