You will generate and rework music, split a track into instruments, record a voiceover, create sound effects and particles, transform a video with AI and stack post-processing effects. Each generator's price is shown before you run it; particles, the Workshop and local post-processing are free.

The Sound & VFX screen

Open Sound & VFX in the rail. A bar of six sections, each with its own color, leads to a page: Music, SFX, Voiceover, Particle VFX, AI VFX and Post-processing. The Music page has three modes: Generate, Import & split and Workshop.

Split a track into instruments

  1. In Music, Import & split mode, drop an audio file or click Import a track. The sounds you already imported are listed below.
  2. Pick the track: it plays. Choose 4 or 6 stems (vocals, drums, bass, other, guitar, piano).
  3. Split the instruments: the first click gives the price, the second runs it (paid, via fal). Each stem joins the Library and the editor's Sounds drawer.

Rework a sound in the Workshop

  1. Workshop mode, or Open in the Workshop from a track or a voice: the sound becomes the graph's source.
  2. Start from a preset (Radio voice, Lo-fi, Great hall, Voice over music) or add nodes from the palette: equalizer, compressor, echo, reverb, ducking, tempo, pitch…
  3. Link a node's output to the next one's input, and set it in the inspector. Under the canvas, the strip shows the waveform: drag to pick a range.
  4. Listen to ten seconds, then Render to the Library.

The AI (paid) nodes extend the track, regenerate or vary a range, or add a sound effect to it. Their price is shown; a generated range is kept and is not paid again as long as nothing changes before it.

Record a voiceover

  1. On the left, the voice library: search, filter, listen, keep your favorites, or Clone a voice… from your own takes.
  2. In the middle, write the script. With the Eleven v3 model, emotion or acting tags are inserted at the cursor. On the right, the model and its settings.
  3. Generate voice. Each edit creates a version: Trim the take, Fade in and out, Normalize, De-ess, Ducking under music, Enhance the take or Isolate the voice. Then Send to the Editor.

Sound effects: pick a model

The SFX section keeps the bundled catalog and the Generate SFX card. Its Model list offers ElevenLabs direct or via fal, Sonilo, Mirelo and Stable Audio, each with its price and maximum length. Describe the sound, set the length (0 lets the model choose), then Generate: two variants go to the Library. A model via fal runs in two clicks, the first shows the price.

Add sound to a video

  1. In the Montage, select a video clip on the timeline, then open the Sounds drawer and its Generate tab.
  2. The From the video block names the clip. Pick the model: Sonilo, Mirelo, Kling, Hunyuan Foley or ThinkSound; some ask for a description of the sound.
  3. A first click gives the estimate, the second runs it. The sound effect goes to the Library; if the model returns the video with sound, it goes there too, under Renders.

Live particles

  1. Particle VFX section: pick a preset (explosion, smoke, sparks, magic…) or a ready-made sequence.
  2. The preview plays right away, computed on your PC. Set the emitter, the motion and the look in the inspector: every setting changes the preview. The same seed always gives the same animation.
  3. Export a PNG image or Export as WebM with transparency: the render goes to the Library.

AI VFX: relight, remove, reframe…

  1. AI VFX section: pick the source video among your finished renders.
  2. Pick an action. Each card shows in motion what it produces (hover it), its price and what it needs.
  3. Estimate, then run: the first click shows the server estimate, the second, Run for real, runs it. The rendered video goes to the Library.

Stack post-processing effects

  1. Post-processing section: pick a render, an AI VFX result or particles.
  2. Click presets in the catalog (16 mm grain, bloom, glitch, LUT, VHS, cinema bars, duotone…): they stack up in order.
  3. In the stack, drag a link to move it, hide it, set its strength. The before/after shows the effect at the chosen moment.
  4. Render the video: the render runs on your PC, for free, and goes to the Library. Save the stack keeps it for another video.

Generate with AI, describe the look

The Generate with AI section sorts the generators by provider (fal, Decart, ElevenLabs): style, upscaling, frame interpolation, sound effects. Each becomes a paid link of the stack, with its price; without its key it stays selectable and you are told. Rendering a paid stack takes a second click, after the estimate.

In Describe the look, write a sentence ("warm 70s film, a little worn") then Suggest a stack. The suggested stack is never applied on its own: Replace the stack or Add to the stack.

The Sounds drawer

All your sounds live in the Sounds drawer: in the Library (Audio tab) and in the editor (B key). Filter by kind, between your sounds and the bundled catalog, search by name or tag.

Enhance

Equalizer, noise reduction, compressor and normalized loudness in one click, on your PC, no provider.

Search by description

Describe what you want to hear. Needs the local search service, otherwise the button says why it is greyed out.

Preview

Hover a sound to hear it (option to turn on); Space plays the selected row.

Good to know

Split, AI nodes, AI VFX, AI links: a first click arms the action and shows the price, a second one starts it. Nothing goes out without that second click, and everything stays under your spending caps.

The animated thumbnails of the actions and presets play on hover. If your system asks to reduce motion, they stay still.
