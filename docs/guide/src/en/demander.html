You will be able to ask a question about the app without leaving it: Deepotus answers in a few steps, takes you to the right screen and shows the button to click. Two minutes; you need a text engine connected (a key, or Ollama on this PC).

Before you start

The assistant uses a text engine you already have: Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, or a local server such as Ollama. With no engine, it shows you where to add a key. It never runs a paid action for you: it explains and shows, you click.

Ask a question

  1. Press F1, or click Ask (the button marked "?") at the bottom right: the drawer opens.
  2. Pick the Engine at the top of the drawer, or leave Automatic. Each engine says whether it is free or paid.
  3. Type your question, for example "where do I put my key?", then Enter.
  4. The answer fits in a few steps. Below it, depending on the question, buttons: Show me, Go there, Guide.

Get shown the button

  1. Click Show me: if the button is on another screen, the app goes there first.
  2. A glowing cursor glides to the button and circles it, with its name in a bubble. It disappears on your first click.
  3. Click Guide to open the chapter about it, in a new tab.

When the assistant does not know the button

  1. The answer offers Look at the screen. Click it only if you agree.
  2. The drawer says which engine will see the capture and what it costs. Only engines that read images are offered.
  3. Click Capture and send: your browser asks to share the tab; choose this tab. Cancel sends nothing.
  4. The assistant answers and the cursor shows the place it found.

If the place found is under the drawer, the drawer closes to show it. If you have changed screens since the capture, the button no longer points: it sends you to the guide.

Dictate and listen

  1. Click the mic next to Send and speak; click it again, or Esc, to stop. The text lands in the input: read it over before sending.
  2. Under an answer, the speaker button reads it with the voice chosen in Settings. With Voicebox, it is free.

Good to know

A question costs a fraction of a cent with a paid engine, counted in the "help" cap of Settings › Pricing & budget. With Ollama on this PC, it is free. Looking at the screen costs a little more: the price shows first.

Dictation through the browser is free. When it goes through the server, the app shows the duration and the cost before sending anything.

The screen capture goes to the chosen engine. Close anything you do not want to show first.
