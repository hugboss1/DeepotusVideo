You will learn to follow what your competitors' customers say (Watch tab), keep your list of prospects (Prospects tab) and let the Campaigns agent find them, write to them and follow their replies. Nothing goes out without your approval, and the app enforces the law. Fifteen minutes; the watch and the records are free, web search, Apify collections and WhatsApp template messages are paid.

Before you start

Everything happens in the F1 drawer, Watch and Prospects tabs, for the workspace chosen in their list (see Work for several brands). The agent that searches and writes is the Campaigns agent (see Run a campaign with the agent): it needs an Anthropic key. Emails go out from your mailbox or Brevo, WhatsApp messages from the workspace's number (WhatsApp screen). Apify collections need an Apify key, set in Settings › API keys.

Watching competitors

  1. Open the Watch tab. It shows the workspace's latest watch: the competitors × themes matrix, the most cited pains, message angles and requests to decide.
  2. Start a collection, or ask the agent "run the watch": Hacker News, YouTube comments (yt-dlp must be installed on the PC) and App Store reviews, read-only, for free, 15 minutes at most.
  3. To go further, the agent collects through Apify: Reddit, X, TikTok, Instagram, Google Maps and Google Play reviews, Trustpilot, G2. Each collection is paid ($2 at most) and goes through a quote card. Paste the addresses to follow into the conversation; authors are anonymised and the reviews join the latest watch.
  4. The What competitors are doing section shows their active ads, the pricing pages that changed (free re-read) and their rank on the searches you follow. Ask "re-read their pricing pages" or "check their ads".
  5. Quotes are evidence to read, never instructions. A public post that states a need never opens a record: the agent suggests a useful public reply, which you post yourself, once.

The Prospects records

  1. Prospects tab › Add a prospect: the source (type, date, address for a web page) and the legal basis are required. A consumer webmail address cannot use B2B legitimate interest.
  2. Statuses follow the pipeline: Suggested, Qualified, Contacted, Replied, Meeting, Client (signed), Lost, Objected. Only allowed moves are offered. Board shows every record in columns.
  3. At Meeting, the app creates a "demo" workspace: it never publishes and is used for the showcase campaign (see below).
  4. People's rights: Export their data, Delete the record, Objects to prospecting. An opt-out applies to all your workspaces; messages prepared for that person are emptied at once.

Let the agent find and qualify

  1. Ask "find me prospects": the agent suggests records from inbound WhatsApp contacts who agreed, a CSV file you drop (waiting list, form: dated consent), or a company page you paste. The French company register (free) gives targets, without records: their website is needed next.
  2. A web search for companies is paid: the agent shows a quote card, nothing runs before your click. The provider and its keys are set in Prospects › Web search.
  3. For local businesses, ask for example "find video studios in Lyon on Google Maps": a quote card ($1 at most). Once accepted, the agent lists name, website and rating, without records, then reads each website and keeps only a published business email. On Reddit, it picks up posts looking for a solution ("alternative to…"): signals you answer publicly, never in private.
  4. Records suggested by the agent say "suggested by the agent". The score starts from a fixed scale, which the agent may adjust by ±20 with a reason. A record found on the web shows "To inform before …" (one month, GDPR art. 14).

Search with Apify

Apify runs collectors (“Actors”) on its own servers: there is nothing to install. The app only calls its list of allowed Actors, always through a card you accept, and Apify stops each collection at its cap ($1 for companies, $2 for the watch). Everything belongs to the brand workspace chosen at the top of the drawer. Once a collection has run, the Actor shows up in your Apify console (Actors › Recently used), with its cost.

  1. Create a token in the Apify console (Settings › API & Integrations), then paste it in Settings › API keys, Apify line, and click Save: it goes to the vault and travels with your transfers.
  2. Prospects › Web search then says “Apify: ready”, with the caps.
  1. Open Campaigns in the brand's workspace and ask in plain words, by type of business and city: “find advertising agencies in Bordeaux on Google Maps, 50 at most”.
  2. By keywords, people looking for a solution: “search Reddit for people looking for an alternative to CapCut for product videos”. These are signals: you reply publicly, never in private.
  3. The card shows the source, the number and the estimated cost. “Collect” starts the collection, Decline commits nothing.
  4. The companies arrive with their website and rating, without records. Then ask “read the website of … and suggest the record”: only a business email published on the website is kept.
  1. To hear a competitor's customers, paste the address to follow into the conversation (their Trustpilot, G2 or Instagram page, their Google Maps reviews), then “run a Trustpilot collection on Runway”. Reddit, X and TikTok are searched with the competitor's name, no address needed. Reviews join the latest watch, authors anonymised.
  2. To see what they do: paste their pricing page address, then “re-read their pricing pages” (free); their Facebook page address, then “check their active ads”; “follow these searches: ai video generator, ai product video”, then “check our rankings”.
  3. The result shows in Watch › What competitors are doing: active ads, what changed on the pricing page, rank on your searches.

Writing: sequences and gates

  1. Once the record is Qualified (or Contacted), ask the agent for a sequence. It drafts it: a first message and up to two follow-ups, on a channel the record allows, in the workspace's voice and without its forbidden words.
  2. This is gate 2: Approve the sequence, in the agent's card or in the record's Sequences block. Then Schedule: each message lands within the workspace's sending hours (the WhatsApp agent's hours), emails under the daily cap.
  3. X, Instagram, LinkedIn: the app never sends private messages. At the planned time, Copy and open copies the text and opens the profile; you send it, then Mark as sent.
  4. WhatsApp: only contacts who agreed. Follow-ups are Meta-approved templates; free text only goes if the contact wrote within 24 hours. A STOP applies to every number of the workspace and, outside client workspaces, to all prospecting.

The email channel

  1. Prospects › Email: the sender, company name and postal address (required), sending from your mailbox (SMTP) or through Brevo, and reading replies (IMAP). The password is sealed for this Windows session and never shown.
  2. Send a test writes to your own address, never to a prospect.
  3. Every email carries your identity and "reply STOP". Replies are read from the mailbox: a reply moves the record to "Replied", a STOP opts it out. No email goes out while reading fails.
  4. Cap: 10 emails a day the first week, 20 the second, 30 after that (can be set lower). Too many bounces or a spam complaint pause sending; Resume sending restarts it.

Following replies

  1. Ask "who replied?": the agent reads the replies (the email text; for WhatsApp, only the date).
  2. For an email reply, it suggests ONE short reply, on that channel, which you approve like a sequence; or it hands the record back to you.
  3. Price, quote, deadline or contract, complaint, refusal, STOP, or a WhatsApp reply to read: the record goes to To take back and the agent stops writing to it. The badge goes away when you note a contact or change the status.

The showcase campaign

For a prospect at the meeting stage, the agent prepares a showcase campaign in their demo workspace: persona, drafts, visuals, to show what you would do. It never publishes. On the paired phone, the agent screen offers “Showcase campaign” for each demo workspace; on the PC, open a Campaigns conversation in that workspace.

Good to know

The watch, the records, the company register and emails are free; the agent's turns, web search, Apify collections and WhatsApp templates are paid, counted under the "campagnes" category (WhatsApp under its own), within your caps.

The agent never approves a sequence for you, never promises a price or a deadline, and never writes to someone who opted out. A public handle or comment is never reused in a message.

A sequence stops on its own when the prospect replies, books a meeting, signs, is lost, opts out or withdraws consent.
