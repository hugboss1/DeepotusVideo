You will be able to find any image, sound or render, file it in a project, send it to the screen that needs it and clean up without losing anything. Everything is free and stays on your PC, except captions by a vision model (paid, quote shown).

The Library screen

Open Library in the rail. At the top, one tab per kind: Images, Renders, 3D, Sprites, Audio, Favorites and Workbench, where a model's versions are listed in lineage order. Everything the app produces arrives here: generators, Photolab, Vectorlab, uploads, phone.

  1. Source chips say where each asset comes from (Generator, Materials, Uploads, mobile…). One click filters.
  2. Tag chips are counted; “3+” keeps images rated three and up. Each image also gets its dominant colour: the hue chips (twelve hues and neutral) filter by colour.
  3. Sort by Most recent or by size, then Grid or list: the list has ten sortable columns (name, source, size, date, dimensions, tags, rating, favourite, licence, hue) and a row opens the sheet.
  4. Each thumbnail shows the file's size and age; the star sets a favourite without opening the sheet, and the dots recall the rating.

An image's sheet

One click on a thumbnail opens its sheet. From top to bottom you find:

Rating and tags

A rating from 0 to 5, separate from the favourite, and free tags: type, then Add. Search covers tags.

Parent and children

Parent shows the original image and the relation (crop, upscale, retouch…); children appear as clickable thumbnails.

Rights

Licence (suggested from the source), author and link, then Save. An unknown licence is flagged in amber: check it before publishing.

File, recipe, uses

Dimensions, weight, format, dominant colour, source; the generator's recipe (prompt, style, model, seed); and every use: renders, posts, bible, shots, projects.

Review comments

Type, then Comment (or Enter). Each comment has a clickable status: to review, approved, rejected; for a sound, the mm:ss moment. They follow the asset when renamed or restored.

Similar images

≈ Similar images shows visually close images, thanks to the CLIP model installed on your PC.

Replay recipe regenerates a generated image with its original recipe, seed included. It is paid: the price is shown and you confirm before the run; the new image arrives at the top of the list.

Send to another screen

  1. In the sheet, click Send to….
  2. Pick the screen: Studio, Quick (start image), Template, Chapter or Workshop, Editor, Cardforge, Sprite Lab, Scheduler… Three targets are specific to images: Photolab to retouch it, Vectorlab which creates a new document at its size, and Tile Lab which makes it a tile's source.
  3. Library project… files the asset in a project: the ones already holding it are ticked (one click removes it), and ＋ New project… creates one.

File by project

The Project list at the top of the screen looks at a single project: every tab then only shows its assets. ＋ New project creates one; a project can be renamed or deleted without touching its assets.

Active project

Auto-file here: everything you produce next (images, renders, 3D, sprites) is filed in it. The reminder stays on screen while it is active, and one click stops it.

On the phone

Pin to the phone: a single project is pinned, and it is the one the mobile companion shows.

Project status

The Status button sorts its assets into edited, published, printed or unused.

Search

The Search button opens the search bar, with three engines:

  1. Text: captions, tags, prompts, recipes, comments and names, ignoring accents and case. Free, instant.
  2. Captions: a vision model describes images that have none, so you find them better. Paid: the quote is shown (images are sent to Google), the cap is checked, then progress counts the images done.
  3. CLIP: searches by meaning (“a lighthouse at sunset”). Install CLIP downloads about 177 MB once, then indexing and search run on your PC, for free, sending nothing.

The Audio tab mounts the Sounds drawer (tags, filters, sorting, actions) above the upload: see Sound & VFX.

Clean up without losing anything

  1. Deleting an image, a sound or a render moves it to the Trash, never further.
  2. Cleanup shows the weight per kind and groups of exact duplicates. Tick extra copies keeps the used copy (or the oldest); protected copies (favourite, use, project) stay greyed out. Confirm to move them to the trash.
  3. The Trash counts items and their weight, and flags those older than 30 days. Restore puts the asset back with its projects; if its name was taken, it gets a neighbouring name and says so.
  4. Emptying deletes permanently: old items, everything, or a single item, always after confirmation.

Good to know

Favourites are saved by the app, no longer by the browser: they follow you from screen to screen and through a transfer. Old browser favourites were imported once.

Only Replay recipe and vision-model captions cost money: price shown first, spending caps checked. Sorting, text search, CLIP, projects and cleanup are free.
