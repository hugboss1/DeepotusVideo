You will be able to turn an image or a description into a 3D model, with one or several engines at once, then send it to the right 3D Studio screen. The quote is free; generation is paid, with the total shown and confirmed before anything starts.

Generate a model

  1. Open Game Assets in the rail, 3D tab: this is the Create screen, in three columns.
  2. On the right, pick the mode: Image (an image from the Library), Text (a Description) or Multi-image (one model per image). Changing mode unticks the engines that cannot read it.
  3. On the left, under Select models, tick one or more engines: each one produces its own model. An engine's settings wheel unfolds its settings; an empty field keeps the default value.
  4. For a character you will animate, the A-pose Recommended pose makes rigging easier. Only Rodin and Meshy 7.1 can pose from this screen.
  5. Set the Quantity (1 to 4 copies per engine and per image). The Estimated total is recomputed on every change.
  6. Click Generate: a dialog recalls the number of generations and the total, then progress shows in the gallery.

Find and send a model

The middle gallery can be filtered (All, Rigged, Unrigged), searched by name and its grid resized. On a card made from an image, the slider compares the source and the 3D render like a wipe.

Under each card, one button per 3D Studio screen: Prepare, Texture, Rig, Animate or Scenery. One click opens the 3D Studio sub-tab on that model, at the chosen screen. What comes next is in The 3D Studio, screen by screen.

Going further

Several engines at once

Tick two or three engines on the same image to compare the results side by side. The total adds them up; 24 generations at most per click.

The former form

Show the former 3D form unfolds the previous screen, folded away during the transition, for an option not yet carried over here.

Good to know

Paid: each generation is counted under the “moteurs3d” category and goes through your spending caps. The quote is free; nothing leaves without your confirmation. The fal.ai key is required, except for local Hunyuan3D 2.1, which is free.

An A-pose or T-pose asked from an engine that cannot pose is refused at launch, before any spending: choose Rodin or Meshy 7.1, or leave None.
