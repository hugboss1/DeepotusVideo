You will learn to create a Character from a few photos, have that Character replay a video in another setting, and film yourself on your webcam transformed live, with their voice. Twenty minutes. Everything is billed per second: the price shows before each launch and counts against your caps.

Before you start

The Avatar live screen opens from the rail. It needs keys depending on what you do: fal for Recast and the setting, Decart for Live, ElevenLabs for the Character's voice. The free local voice needs an NVIDIA graphics card and Voixbox (see Install and train a local RVC voice).

Only clone your own face and voice, or those of someone who has agreed to it. The app makes you confirm this for every Character and keeps the date.

Create a Character

  1. Characters tab: give the Character a Name.
  2. Drop one to eight photos of the same face, front-on and well lit (512 px recommended). They are the reference for Recast and Live.
  3. Tick the consent box (“I have the right to use this face and this voice…”) then click Create the Character.
  4. Optional: Clone the voice with one to five clips of the voice alone (30 s to 3 min in total). Cloning goes through ElevenLabs.

Recast: replay a video

A 3 to 30 s video keeps its acting; the Character, the setting or an element changes. The render takes a few minutes and joins your renders.

  1. Recast tab: drop the source video, or pick a render from the app.
  2. Choose the Model: its price per second is shown next to it.
  3. Choose the Character, then a setting from the Presets or a free instruction (preferably in English).
  4. Tick the Character's voice if you want the take's voice converted into theirs, at the same pace.
  5. Read the quote under the button, then Start the Recast.

Live: your webcam, transformed

  1. Live tab: the first time, paste your Decart key and click Save the key. It goes to the vault; the page never sees it.
  2. Choose the Character (or keep your face), a Setting, the Format and the Reserved duration.
  3. Choose the Voice sent: your own as it is, the Character's through ElevenLabs (about 2 s delay), or a local RVC voice (under a second).
  4. Start the live session. The transformed picture appears next to your webcam; switch settings on the fly with Apply the setting.
  5. Record the live session keeps the footage in your renders, ready for the Editor or a Recast.

When a voice is converted, the picture is delayed by as much as the voice: lips and sound arrive together. The live bar shows the converted segments, the latency and any losses.

Going further

Background only

The subject of the video is cut out, then placed on a color, a Library image or a video. The acting, the voice and the size do not change.

Change only the voice

Keeps a video's picture and replaces its voice with the Character's. Billed at the ElevenLabs character equivalent.

On the phone

The companion app starts a Recast, films a take or opens a Live session, at your PC's prices and under the same caps.

Good to know

Live is reserved up front, by the minute; only the time actually played is billed. The reserved duration is cut off at Decart even if the page closes.

A tab hidden for 60 s stops the live session, so you don't pay for nothing. Prefer Edge or Chrome: they can delay the picture to match the voice.
