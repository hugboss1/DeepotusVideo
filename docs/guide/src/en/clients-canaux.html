You will be able to create a client's space with verified channels, point each project at its accounts, and publish for the client without picking the wrong account or skipping their consent. Free: verifying a channel publishes nothing and costs nothing. A space's persona, identity and export are in Work for several brands; each network's keys in Connect your accounts.

Create a client's space

  1. Open Settings › Brand spaces, type the name, then Create space: the New space wizard opens, in four steps.
  2. Type and identity: name, colour, brand kit and space type, Brand, Product or Client. A product can belong to a brand or a client: it takes over its kit, its persona and, for a client, its rules.
  3. Client sheet (clients only): company, contact (name, email, WhatsApp), country, language and Publishing mode, approved by the client or published on their behalf.
  4. Channels: tick the networks and enter their credentials; secrets go to the vault and are never shown again. Verify queries the network without publishing anything: verified, undetermined (with the reason) or refused. An unverified channel is still saved.
  5. Summary: the publishing mode, unverified channels and missing consents. Finish activates the space.

Point a project at its channels

  1. In the Library, pick the project in the Project bar, then click Channels.
  2. Tick the space's accounts that will receive its posts, then Save. Their icons show next to the project.
  3. While this project is active, everything created targets these accounts: the Scheduler's New post, Send to › Scheduler in the Library, the agent's drafts, content made from an inspiration, edits of a recording. Untick them in the post editor. With no project channel, X stays the target.

Publish for a client safely

On Quick, the Studio, the Scheduler and the Library, a banner under the header says who you are working for: the space, the active project and, for a client, its name. Client sheet opens their consents, posts awaiting approval and log.

One consent per channel

Each verified channel needs the client's dated consent (in writing, on WhatsApp or signed with a finger on the companion): Add, in the sheet. Revoke pauses the scheduled posts that target that channel.

Approved by the client

A post starts Awaiting approval and can only be scheduled once the answer is recorded: Approved by the client or Needs changes. Changing its text or media cancels the approval.

Published on their behalf

Scheduling a post or sending it right away opens a confirmation that names the client and their channels: Publish on their behalf.

Never the wrong account

A post only goes out on its space's accounts. If an approval, a consent or a confirmation is missing, the app refuses and says which one.

Check a channel again

Under the list of spaces, Publishing accounts shows each account with its badge; hovering gives the date and reason of the last check. Verify runs the check again, still without publishing. Rewriting a credential clears the verification. In a post's editor in the Scheduler, a ticked unverified account says so.

Good to know

Deepotus and your brands behave as before: these rules only apply to Client spaces and the products that belong to them. A client's WhatsApp is linked in the same Channels step: see Talk to your clients on WhatsApp.

Without an active consent on a channel, no post of the client goes out on that channel, even when scheduled. A consent can only be added on a verified channel: verify the account first. A revocation pauses without deleting anything; posts resume as soon as a new consent is active.
