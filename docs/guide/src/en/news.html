You will follow your news feeds, let the free ranking sort today's articles, and go from article to scheduled post with the daily pipeline. Sorting, filtering and preparing are free; AI and illustrated reels are paid, on request only.

The News screen

Open News in the rail. On the left, your sources; in the middle, the ranked articles; on the right, the Compose panel. What you ticked, the query, the sort, the voice, the length and the script are back when you return to the screen.

Choose your sources

  1. Add: paste the address of an RSS or Atom feed. Defaults restores the bundled pack (crypto, geopolitics, economy, politics).
  2. A source's switch pauses it without deleting it.
  3. Refresh all re-reads every feed. The screen also does it on its own, once a day. Pickups of the same wire story are merged into a single article.

Today's ranking

By default, articles are sorted by Today’s score, a free score: your brief's words, pickup by other outlets, a readable article. The reason and the score show on each card; Most recent sorts by date.

Trending

A topic picked up by three outlets the same day is marked trending, in green on the card.

Already covered

A topic close to a reel launched in the last 30 days is marked in amber, title on hover, and moves down the ranking. An overused source moves down too.

Dropped

The dropped chip counts the articles the filter removed; hover it for the reasons. Show all shows the full ranking, Top only goes back to the first five.

Rank with AI

Rank with AI re-ranks the batch with a language model: paid, under your caps, and only when you click.

In Compose, Campaign brief says what matters today and steers the score. Free filter sets the keywords (empty: everything passes), blocked words and sources, and freshness in hours (1 to 720); Save filter re-ranks at once.

From article to post: the daily pipeline

  1. Click Daily pipeline. Pick the reel format, the number of articles and the channels. Each format's cost shows before any run: animated cards are free, rendered on your PC; AI illustration, video shots, avatar and voice-over are paid.
  2. Prepare (free) assembles the batch without any paid call: ranked articles, draft script, caption ending with its sources (outlet, date, domain), Scheduler slot.
  3. If needed, Polish with AI rewrites the script (paid). If the spending cap stops the polishing, the draft is kept.
  4. Review the script, caption and slot, then Schedule batch: the post lands in the Scheduler, with its cards reel rendered for free. Nothing is written or spent before that click.

Going further

X signal

X signal reads recent X posts on your brief: on click only, three times a day at most, counted in your X read quota.

Voice by topic

With Auto (by topic), the persona's tone is picked from the topic's words, for free, and the reason is shown.

To the Studio

Tick articles, then Send to Studio to build them yourself in a graph (see Compose with the Studio).
