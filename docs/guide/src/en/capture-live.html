You will be able to record the app with your voice, your camera and your clicks, dress it up with a skin, turn it into an edited video, go live on Twitch, YouTube or Kick, and share a tutorial that others replay. Everything is free except voice transcription, on a quote; nothing leaves the PC without your action.

Record the app

  1. In the top bar, click Record the app. Choose what is filmed: The app only, without the boxes laid over it such as the Ask drawer, or The whole tab.
  2. Sound: Record my voice (microphone) and, if needed, the app's sound. The camera only turns on with the consent box; its floating inset can be moved and resized.
  3. Clicks: the click track (never the keys you type), a halo and, if you like, a live zoom. Then Start recording and share this tab when the browser asks; another tab is refused.
  4. While recording, the REC badge shows the microphone and the camera. Keys, vault, pairing, devices and passwords are hidden in the picture.
  5. Stop saves the video and its raw tracks in the Library, on this PC.

Dress the video with a skin

In the recording or live box, the Look line picks the skin; Edit skins opens the editor. Five skins are built in: Deepotus, Plain, Neon, Press and Gaming. Each space has its own, Deepotus by default.

  1. Pick a skin: the preview plays on a sample capture. Drag the logo, the lower third, the alerts, the signature or the camera inset to place them.
  2. Set colours and fonts (the space kit's by default), frame and background, lower third, intro and outro, live alerts, break and end screens, zoom and cursor. Play the intro or Break screen show the element.
  3. A built-in skin is edited as a copy: Save a copy, then Use for this space.

Edit the recording

  1. At the end of a recording, click Edit the recording; later, the Record the app box offers Edit a recording….
  2. The edit is suggested from the track: zooms on clicks, generation dead time to Speed up or Cut, one chapter per screen change. Adjust them, or add Zoom at this moment and Chapter at this moment.
  3. Format: 16:9, 9:16 (the frame follows the zooms) or 1:1. Subtitles: a known text synced to the voice, free, or a transcription, paid after a quote.
  4. Render the video: it goes into the Library, its chapters ready for a YouTube description. Schedule a draft puts it in the Scheduler, on the active project's channels; nothing is published.

Go live

  1. In the top bar, click Live and give the live a title.
  2. + Add a destination: Twitch, YouTube Live, Kick or an RTMP server, which also covers Facebook, LinkedIn or Restream. Paste the stream key, then Save to the vault: it is never shown again.
  3. Discord does not take another app's video: added through its webhook, it announces the live and its links in the chosen channel.
  4. Tick the ready destinations, the camera if you wish, the skin, then Go live.
  5. The panel tracks the stream's health per destination. Break screen shows "be right back", Marker notes a moment for editing, Stop ends it. The live is also recorded in the Library.

Share a replayable tutorial

  1. In a recording's edit editor, Tutorial… suggests one step per gesture and one chapter per screen, with a title and a short sentence in French and English. Keep the useful steps and touch up the texts.
  2. Build the drafts prepares, without publishing anything, the step pictures for the guide and the lab cards.
  3. Export the walkthrough produces a .dzparcours file: the video, the click track and the steps, with no typed text nor personal path.
  4. On another installation, Record the app › Walkthroughs… › Import a walkthrough…, then Replay. Show me points at the real button without clicking for you; an element missing from that installation is flagged.

Good to know

The Deepotus skin signs the video with a small "Made with DeepotusVideo": untick it in the editor if you prefer. A skin is shared as a .zip: Export (.zip) and Import a skin (.zip).

A running recording prevents going live: stop it first. In The app only mode, boxes laid over the app are not filmed; choose The whole tab to show them.
