You will be able to link a space's WhatsApp number, answer your contacts with the help of an agent, have the client approve a post and broadcast a post to your contacts. Linking is free; Meta charges for messages, the agent's drafts are paid, and every send goes through the space's cap.

Before you start

WhatsApp goes through Kapso, a layer over Meta's official API that receives messages in place of the PC. Its key, a single one for all spaces, is set in Settings › API keys. Each client links their own Meta Business account and number; one number serves one space only.

Link a space's number

  1. In the space wizard (see Work for clients), Channels step, tick WhatsApp. It is not added from the list of accounts: it only shows there to be verified or deleted.
  2. For a client, first record their consent to the link: how and when it was given.
  3. Choose the Meta billing: by default the client pays Meta with their own card; otherwise Kapso pays with your credits (+5 %) and you re-invoice, if partner billing is enabled at Kapso. This choice is final.
  4. Create the link (free): a Kapso setup link appears, valid for 30 days. Copy it, then send it to the client your usual way: the app sends nothing. They connect their Facebook account there in a few minutes.
  5. Verify reads the link again: once the client has finished, the linked number shows.

Reply on the WhatsApp page

The WhatsApp entry in the rail opens the active space's conversations, with five tabs: Conversations, Templates, Contacts, Health and Meta prices.

  1. Pick a conversation. Its badge says whether the 24-hour window is open; once closed, only templates approved by Meta can go out: Send a template.
  2. When the agent has drafted, the Reply proposed by the agent waits: fix it, then Send or Reject. Draft a reply asks it for one.
  3. Take over silences the agent on this conversation; Hand back gives it back.

Set up the WhatsApp agent

In Settings › Brand spaces, the WhatsApp agent button, under the active space's accounts, opens its settings: Agent on, mode, roles (common questions, appointments, orders with no price or payment, post approval), hours and out-of-hours message, limits per contact and per day, words that call a person, monthly cap. It speaks with the space's persona and respects its banned words.

Drafts, by default

The agent drafts; you review and send each reply from the WhatsApp page.

Automatic

Once confirmed, the agent replies on its own, within hours and limits. A reply about money, a negative tone or a request for a person stays a draft to take over.

Approval by the client

With the approval role ticked, Send to the client on WhatsApp, in the Client sheet, sends them the post preview with two buttons: approve or ask for changes. Their answer comes back dated in the sheet.

Broadcast a post on WhatsApp

  1. In a post's inspector in the Scheduler, under Publishing accounts, tick the space's WhatsApp account, one per post. A project's channels never tick it on their own.
  2. The WhatsApp broadcast panel asks for a template approved by Meta (no media header nor variable text header, never authentication) and its parameters, the same for everyone.
  3. It shows the audience, meaning the contacts whose consent is recorded, minus STOP and marketing opt-outs, and the quote. Save the broadcast spends nothing.
  4. The broadcast goes out when the post is published, after the cap check; the client's approval applies as for any post. Then the panel tracks sent, delivered, read, failures and actual cost: Refresh.

Keep your contacts and prices

Consents and STOP

Contacts tab: Record consent, with its source, before any first message. A contact who writes STOP gets nothing more; Lift the STOP requires the source of the opt-in.

Meta prices

Meta charges each delivered message by category and country. Enter the grid by market, then Save the grid: an unknown price makes the send refused.

Health

The month's messages, delivery failures, templates rejected by Meta and the agent's state.

Good to know

Messages count under the whatsapp category, the agent's drafts under whatsapp_agent. The Kapso plan is shared by all spaces: each agent's monthly message cap keeps one client from eating the others' quota.

If a broadcast's outcome is uncertain, Confirm the send retries it after a confirmation box: it is paid, some contacts may receive it twice, and the post also goes out again on its other channels.
