You will find your way around the app, move from one screen to another without searching, and read what the top bar tells you. Five minutes, free.

Deepotus Video Gen is a studio that runs on your own computer. It turns an idea into a video, a picture, a 3D object or a published post. You plug in your own AI provider keys: you pay those providers, not the app. Everything you create stays with you.

The screen at a glance

  1. The rail, on the left: one screen per task. Quick for a one-click video, Studio to compose, Edit to cut a video, Library to find your media, Settings for the keys. The arrow at the top of the rail folds it into icons only.
  2. The quick command: click Quick command… or press Ctrl+K, type a few letters, Enter.
  3. The spending counter shows what you spent this month with your providers. One click opens Settings.
  4. The badges fal, heygen and voice turn green when the matching key is set. One click takes you to that key.
  5. The render queue, top right, lists what is being computed and what just finished, in the Running and Recent tabs.

Go fast with the quick command

The quick command goes to any screen (Go to Studio, Go to News…), opens the last render, creates a starter graph or opens a Settings section. Replay onboarding restarts the first-launch tour.

What the app does to protect you

Nothing leaves without you

Only this PC can change, generate or spend. Another device on the network can only read, and only once paired.

Errors that make sense

A missing key or a server refusal is shown in plain words, with what to do.

Safe imports

A picture with the same name becomes name-1, name-2…; a file that is not a real picture is refused and named.

In-app dialogs

Confirmations and text prompts use one Deepotus dialog, never a browser pop-up.

Good to know

The desktop icon opens the app at http://deepotus.localhost:8765. Each browser tab carries the name of the open tool, for example “Deepotus — Vectorlab”.

Only AI generations cost money, and your provider bills them. The chapter Keep spending under control shows how to set limits.
