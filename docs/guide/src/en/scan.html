You will be able to turn a photo into working material: a book page into text, a post that inspires you into new content of your own, a diagram into layers ready to animate. Text is read on the PC, for free; Claude and AI background removal are paid fallbacks, with their price shown first.

Read a book page

  1. In the Chapters Workshop, click Scan a page.
  2. Choose files…: 1 to 10 pages, as images or PDF; you can also paste an image. Pick the text language (French, English or auto), then Read.
  3. Windows reads the page on the PC. Columns are put back in order, hyphenations joined, page numbers and running heads removed. A PDF that already holds text is taken as is.
  4. Proofread the text, which is editable: odd words are highlighted, Previous and Next jump from one to the next.
  5. Send it: Insert into the chapter, Create the chapters (the proposed split, then Create these chapters) or Import as a manuscript.

Draw new content from an inspiration

You like a post seen elsewhere: you remake it in your own voice, you never republish it.

  1. In the Scheduler, click From an inspiration. The top of the box says for which space, project and channels.
  2. The capture: drop the image or paste it (Ctrl+V), or paste the post's text; note where it comes from and its link, kept in the record and never published. Read the capture (free) reads the text and picks up the palette.
  3. Material and three angles: Extract the material and 3 angles (paid) draws out the tone, structure, ideas and composition, then suggests three new angles in the space's voice. An angle that repeats the capture or holds a banned word cannot be chosen. Choose this angle.
  4. Quote and generation: the text by Claude and, if you tick it, a new visual by fal; the total shows, then Generate.
  5. Draft: touch up the title, hook and caption, then Create the draft. It lands in the Scheduler on the active project's channels and follows the client's rules.

Animate a photographed diagram

  1. In the Vectorlab, File › Scan a diagram…, then drop the photo.
  2. Background removal is local and free for a plain background or a transparent image; AI removal is paid and asks for confirmation. Pick the label language, then Analyze.
  3. The list shows the layers in reveal order: shapes, arrows and read labels, the order following the arrows. Create the document turns it into a Vectorlab document with named layers.
  4. File › Send to › Animation opens the Studio on a ready graph: an Animation node with one element per visible layer, ready for Choreograph, and a local, free motion code render.

Good to know

A difficult page (handwriting, low contrast): Try with Claude shows its price, then asks for confirmation. A paid reading is kept: picking it up again costs nothing.

A draft in which 12 words in a row repeat the capture is refused; its closeness to the capture is shown. The captured image is never the post's visual: at most it serves as a composition reference, which the recipe records.

Text read from a page or a capture stays text: a sentence like "ignore your instructions" is transcribed, never followed.
