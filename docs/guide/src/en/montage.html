You will place your shots on a timeline, cut them, move them and chain them, with the gestures of a professional editor. Free: editing and previews run on your PC.

The editor screen

At the bottom, the timeline: titles (T1), overlays (V2), main video (V1), then dialogue (A1), music (A2), sound effects (A3) and subtitles (S1). In the centre, the viewer; on the right, the inspector of the selected shot. At the top, Preview (free 480p preview), Render and Publish. The bottom bar switches between three views: Media, Edit and Delivery.

Place shots

  1. Open the media drawer: all your finished video renders, with thumbnail, length and origin. Filter by origin (Studio, Chapters, Quick…), by title or by rating (three stars and up).
  2. Click a media item to place it at the playhead. The edit mode decides how: overwrite, insert and push the rest, at the end, above, ripple overwrite, or fill the range.
  3. From the Library, Send to › Edit also places a video on V1 with its sound on A1. A Studio render or a Chapters episode opens straight in the editor.

Cut and adjust

Cut

The blade (Alt+C) cuts the shot at the playhead. A gap is selected with one click and Del closes it.

Roll, slip, slide

Alt + drag the diamond moves the cut; Alt + drag the centre slips the content; Shift + drag moves the shot between its neighbours.

Range and markers

I and U set an in and out range; Shift+X cuts it on every track. Shift+M sets a marker, Ctrl+M lists them.

Everything undoes

Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y over fifty steps: shots, mix, tracks, subtitles, range, markers.

Cut at scene changes

Right-click a clip: the app spots each scene change and cuts it for you.

Shots too long

In the menu › View, the timeline highlights shots that run too long and jump cuts from the same source.

Chain two shots

  1. Click the diamond between two shots: the gallery opens, with 58 transitions sorted by family (fades, slides, wipes, zooms, pixels, shapes).
  2. The same panel shows the last frame of the left shot and the first of the right one; shift the cut frame by frame.
  3. For a quick fade on the selected shot: Alt+T. Simple fades play live; the others after a Preview.

Organise your projects

The menu lists every action in six sections, with its shortcut. Projects… opens the grid of your projects: open, duplicate, rename, delete, or compare two versions. Before opening another one, an unnamed edit is put away safely on its own.

New edit

An empty project, with its default tracks, and nothing else.

Copy between projects

Ctrl+C on a clip, Ctrl+V in another project.

Auto-clips

From a long video, AI suggests 1 to 8 excerpts of 15 to 60 s, then creates a project with the chosen one (paid; a free version without AI exists).

Your shortcuts

The ? panel lists the shortcuts; a “Resolve” preset aligns them with DaVinci Resolve. Export them and import them back.
