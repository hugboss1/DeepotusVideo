You will be able to create seamless materials in Material Forge, from a free catalogue, a generator, a photo or a description, and export them to Blender, Unity, Unreal or Godot. The catalogue, generators and maps are free and local; only the AI-generated base image is billed.

A material from the catalogue

  1. Open the Materials tab of the Game Assets hub, then CC0 catalog: thirty free Poly Haven materials in six families (floors, walls and bricks, metals, wood, fabrics and leathers, concrete and terrain).
  2. Pick one or more then import: missing maps are derived locally, the source credit is kept.
  3. The material lands in the gallery with a real 3D preview, its eight PBR maps and its measured seam score. One click reopens it in the editor.

The generators

The Generators tab offers ten seamless patterns: bricks, tiles, planks, checkerboard, hexagons, pebbles, brushed metal, leather, fabric, stripes. Tune them live, then Create the material. Local and free.

Forge from a description or a photo

  1. Forge tab: describe the material (rusty iron, frosted glass…) or start from a reference image; pick the resolution (1K, 2K, 4K) and the seam method.
  2. Forge the material: only the base image is billed (price shown on the button); seams, maps and preview are computed locally, recomputable at will.
  3. A raw photo becomes a material: click the four corners of a surface shot at an angle to straighten it, then remove the baked lighting with an adjustable strength (the badge shows the before and after gap). On a phone, the field opens the camera.

Judge and tune

Environments

Seven HDRIs provided, and your own (.hdr or 2:1 image) to add or delete. Varied gallery meshes: sphere, cube, torus, cylinder, plane, 3×3 tiling.

Compare, My model

Compare opens a second preview, same environment and camera; “My model” puts the material on a Workbench model.

Physical height

The height map declares its millimetres (0 to 20 mm), reused by the card 3D Forge's relief.

Finishes

A preset (lacquer, leather, animated emissive, brushed metal) is tried in the preview before being applied or cancelled. Emission above 1 keeps its hue.

Export

Each material exports as a ZIP with a manifest, following a naming convention: standard, Blender (Principled BSDF slots indicated), Unity, Unreal or Godot. It also serves as a source in Tile Lab and as a material in the 3D Forge.

Good to know

Only the base image of “Forge the material” is billed, within your caps. Catalogue, generators, photo, delighting, maps and exports are free.
