You will be able to take a 3D model from start to finish in the 3D Studio: lighten it, texture it, give it a skeleton, fix its skin, animate it and export it to a game, the web or a printer. Almost everything is local and free; the AI steps are paid, on a confirmed quote.

Finding your way: the screen bar

The 3D Studio is the 3D Studio sub-tab of Game Assets. Its bar has three groups: Open and Import; then the model's journey, from Prepare to Export, greyed out while no model is open; finally Scenery and Graph, the former Meshy graph.

  1. Open shows all your models (generated, imported, retouched), with filters and search. A click on a thumbnail opens the model in Prepare.
  2. Import takes a .glb, .gltf, .obj, .stl, .fbx or .vrm file. FBX goes through Blender and is refused if Blender is missing.
  3. The Open banner recalls the model, its bones, vertices, triangles and version. Every operation writes a new version; the old one stays.
  4. The banner's “Send to …” button moves to the next screen of the journey, without saving anything.

Prepare: lighten the model

The sheet gives vertices, triangles, meshes, materials and bones. A preset, from Micro prop to Max detail, or the slider sets the target. Re-optimize · new version decimates locally and opens the result; Both compares original and optimised side by side. Back to the original (v1) deletes nothing. To repair or cut, Open in the Workbench leads to the Workbench screen, on the same model (see the Workbench).

Texture: repaint the model

  1. Reference panel: aim at a view and a lighting, then Frame. The dotted frame shows what will go to the image model.
  2. Generate sends the capture to the chosen image model and stores the image in the references rail.
  3. Retexture has Meshy repaint the mesh from the chosen reference or a style text, as a new version.
  4. Projection panel: Run auto texture captures 4 to 18 views, has them painted and projects them onto the UV atlas. Eraser, smudge, fill, Fill all gaps and Heal retouch locally, then Save as a version.

Rig: give it a skeleton

The 22-bone template fits the model. Adjust joints lets you drag them with the gizmo, with Mirror and Children; Shift+click picks several. Recompute weights writes a rigged version, for free. Meshy auto-rig does the job by AI, paid, after confirmation.

Refine: fix the skin

  1. A test pose, such as Arms raised, shows where the skin deforms. Bind pose returns to rest.
  2. Pick the bone with Select bone (1), then Paint (2) or Lasso (3), with the Set, Add, Subtract, Smooth or Blur operation.
  3. Or Fix everything: shoulders, limbs, seams and healing over a battery of poses, in one version.
  4. Save weights writes your retouches into a new version.

Animate: bring it to life

Generated plays the model's clips. Catalogue browses 680 Meshy animations, with starred items and collections; “Apply” puts them on the model, paid. Generate turns a text into motion, also paid. Import a motion retargets a BVH or an animated GLB onto your skeleton, for free. Drive mode walks the character with the keyboard (ZQSD, WASD or arrows).

Export: deliver

One button per target, all local and free: Skeletal mesh only · .glb, Pose · .glb, Animation · .glb, FBX via Blender, Godot 4 · .tscn scene + hitboxes, Unreal, Unity, three.js package · .zip, LOD chain · .zip, PBR textures · .zip and STL + 3MF in millimetres. The links arrive in Ready files. Save to Library makes it one more model.

Good to know

Paid, counted under the “moteurs3d” category: the reference image, auto texture, AI denoising, retexture, auto-rig, applying an animation and text to motion. Each one shows a quote, then asks for confirmation. Everything else is local and free.

Nothing is overwritten: every screen writes one more version. A click on a version, in Prepare or Export, reopens the model as it was.

“Close the model” (×) asks for no confirmation, even with unsaved changes: save first.
