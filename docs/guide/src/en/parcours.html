You will be able to chain screens to go from an idea to a published or printed result. Each path points to the chapters that detail every step; any cost is announced before each run.

An image, then a post

  1. In the Library, describe the image then Create image (paid, price shown), or upload your own.
  2. Retouch it if needed in Photolab: Send to… › Photolab, then Save.
  3. Send to… › Scheduler creates a post draft; the Scheduler sets caption, channels and slot. Nothing goes out without your click.

A reel from the news

  1. In News, let the free ranking sort today's articles.
  2. Daily pipeline: pick the reel form (animated cards are free), then Prepare (free).
  3. Review the script and caption, then Schedule batch: the post lands in the Scheduler with its reel.

An episode of your novel

  1. In Chapters, paste your chapter; the Workshop derives the breakdown, the bible and the storyboard.
  2. Block the tricky shots on the 3D Stage before paying for any video.
  3. Assemble the episode, finish it in the Editor (titles, mix, subtitles), then deliver in each network's formats.

A printed card game

  1. In Card Forge, start from a template, import your data (CSV, Google Sheets, Notion) and set the format.
  2. Retouch a face in Vectorlab if needed (“Edit this face in the Vectorlab”).
  3. Module 07: pick your printer's template, let preflight re-read the file, then export the printer pack. The 3D Forge adds tokens and a box.

A 3D-printed figurine

  1. Pick a cleanly cut-out image and generate the model in the 3D tab of Game Assets (paid, price shown), or prepare its views for free first.
  2. Open it in the Workbench: target size, repair, lay down, check overhangs, arrange on the bed.
  3. Export › 3D printing, then Open in the slicer: see 3D-print your creations.

A campaign for a client

  1. Create the client's space and verify its channels: Work for clients. Link their WhatsApp in the same place: Talk to your clients on WhatsApp.
  2. Start from a post that inspires you: Scan a page, an inspiration, a diagram. The draft lands in the Scheduler on the project's channels.
  3. Have the client approve it, then schedule it; the WhatsApp broadcast goes out with it.

A video tutorial of the app

  1. Record your gesture, dress it with a skin and edit the recording: Record the app and go live.
  2. Schedule the video's draft in the Scheduler, and export the walkthrough so someone else can replay it.

Assets for your game

  1. Animate a character in Sprite Lab and export the sheet for Unity, Godot or Phaser.
  2. Build a tileset in Tile Lab from two materials, paint a map, export to Tiled, LDtk or Godot.
