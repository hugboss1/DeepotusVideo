You will learn to turn a Reel that inspires you into a Reel of your own: measure its form, draw a recipe from it without copying anything, write your script with your offer, then render it as animated panels. Measurements and rendering are free; reading the voice and writing the script are paid ≈ $, on a quote.

Before you start

You need a screen recording of the reference video, with sound on: the application never downloads someone else's video. A link alone gives the title, the author and, on YouTube, the public subtitles; it is not enough for a recipe. Reading and the script need an Anthropic key (see Connect your keys and AI engines).

1. Drop the video and measure it

  1. In the Scheduler, click From an inspiration. The box says which space and channels you are working for.
  2. Drop the recording in the drop zone, or paste a link in the Link (kept in the record, never published) field.
  3. Click Read the capture (free). The Reference sheet gives the length, frame, frame rate, sound, number of shots and their pace. A capture that repeats is measured on its real length.

2. Read the voice and write the recipe

  1. Click “Read the voice and the screen, then write the recipe (≈ …)” and confirm the amount. The voice is transcribed, then Claude reads at most six contact sheets.
  2. The Recipe splits the video into timed bricks: hook, problem, proof, offer, call… with their visual, the subtitle style and the call to action. Its numbers come from the measurements, not from the model.
  3. Any passage too close to the source is removed. The recipe is stored in the space and in My recipes; it holds no sentence or image from the source.

3. Fill in the offer sheet

The script talks about your product, not the source's. The first time, the box opens the space's offer sheet: Product or service, For whom, Promise, True proofs (one per line), objection removed, and the publishable figures with their source. Tick Allow the "comment a word" call to action if you want to reply to keyword comments (see Grow your posts). Then Save the offer sheet.

4. Write and approve the script

  1. Click “Write the script with my offer (≈ …)” and confirm. Claude writes one sentence per brick, with as many words as the source, and the on-screen text.
  2. The checks show: Hook within 3 s, One idea per line, Ends on a call to action, Real, sourced figures, Distance from the source, Forbidden words.
  3. Fix it if needed, then Approve the script (gate 1). An unsourced figure, a copy or a forbidden word blocks the approval.

5. Render the Reel in panels

  1. In Reel in panels, click Prepare the cue sheet: one panel per brick, animated on the word timings, in the space's colours.
  2. Click Render the Reel (local, free) and follow the render in the Job Dock. The provenance is noted in the render's recipe and in the MP4 comment.
  3. Create the draft in the Scheduler stores the Reel in assisted mode: nothing goes out without you. The public mention of the source is offered unticked.

Going further

My recipes

A recipe can be reused for another script or another product. It keeps the date, the source and the pace, never the original text.

Illustrative value

A figure or chart without a source in your offer is labelled “illustrative value” on screen. Add its source to the sheet to remove it.

Good to know

The source video and its contact sheets are deleted seven days after the analysis; the sheet stays. The transcript becomes a fingerprint used only to check that nothing is copied.

Reading and the script are counted under the “campagnes” category, after you confirm the amount shown. See Keep spending under control.
