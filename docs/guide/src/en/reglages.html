You will find any setting, give your content a voice with personas and dress the app in your colours with brand kits. Ten minutes, free.

Find a setting

  1. Open Settings in the rail.
  2. Type a word in Search settings…: accents and capitals do not matter.
  3. Enter opens the first result, Esc clears the field.

Give a voice with personas

A persona is a personality: its tone, its audience, its way of speaking. The active persona sets the News scriptwriter, the tone of the prompt generator and the default voiceover.

  1. Open Settings › Personas.
  2. Built-in personas can be read but not edited: duplicate one, or click New persona.
  3. Click a persona to make it active.

Dress the app with a brand kit

  1. Open Settings › Branding. The kit list shows which one is active.
  2. New kit creates one; Activate makes it the active kit and refreshes the app.
  3. The fields below edit the active kit: Logo…, app name, sub-label, two tagline lines, primary and accent colour. Save brand.
  4. Rename, Duplicate and Delete manage each kit. The active kit and the last kit cannot be deleted: the greyed-out button says why.

The active kit dresses the splash screen, the sidebar, the app's accents and Template renders that use the brand. Reset to deepotus restores the original look.

Good to know

A Template render keeps the kit that was active when you sent it, even if you switch kits while it computes.

Settings › Appearance › Reduced motion turns off decorative animations; it also follows the Windows setting.
