You will be able to open an image in Photolab, select it, retouch it, adjust it and send it back to the Library or another screen. Free and offline: every gesture is computed on your PC by the photocraft engine shipped with Deepotus, with the same pixels as the original application.

Open an image

  1. Open Photolab in the app bar, right after Vectorlab. From the Library, Send to… › Photolab opens the image directly.
  2. With no document, the welcome screen offers New… (Ctrl+N) and Open from the Library… (Ctrl+O). You can also drag a file onto the work area: it enters the Library and opens.
  3. A new document is chosen by category (Photo, Film and video, Mobile, Print, Web) and preset: HDTV 1080p, 4K UHD, Instagram square, Story 9:16, A4 300 ppi…
  4. Each document has its tab; a dot means it has changed. The status bar at the bottom gives the zoom, mode and depth (RGB · 8 bits), size in pixels and number of layers.

The screen follows the Deepotus language, French or English: menus, tools, panels, dialogs and history.

Pick a tool

The toolbar on the left has twenty slots. A slot marked with a corner holds several tools: right-click or long-press to choose. Each tool has its letter (V, M, L, W, C, I, J, B, S, Y, E, G, O, P, T, A, U, H, Z), and the same letter cycles to the next tool in the group. The strip at the top shows the active tool's settings.

Select

Rectangle and ellipse (M), lasso and polygonal lasso (L), magic wand, quick selection and object selection (W). Shift adds, Alt subtracts.

Crop and measure

Crop (C): draw the frame, Enter to cut. The eyedropper (I) picks a colour; the ruler, count and notes are in the next chapter.

Paint

Brush, pencil and mixer brush (B), eraser and background eraser (E), gradient and paint bucket (G). [ and ] set the size, a digit the opacity (4 then 5 = 45%).

Repair

Spot healing and healing brush (J), clone stamp (S, Alt+click for the source), history brush (Y), dodge, burn, sponge, blur, sharpen and smudge (O).

Text and shapes

Type (T): click for a line, drag for a paragraph. Pen (P), path selection (A), rectangle, ellipse, triangle, polygon, line and custom shapes (U).

Move around

Hand (H) or hold Space; zoom (Z) or Ctrl+wheel. Ctrl+0 fits the screen, Ctrl+1 shows 100%.

The swatches under the toolbar hold the foreground and background colours: X swaps them, D restores black and white.

Work the selection

The Select menu holds All (Ctrl+A), deselect (Ctrl+D) and inverse (Ctrl+Shift+I). The engine can also find the subject, the sky, a colour range or the focus area. Modify › Border, Smooth, Expand, Contract and Feather refine the outline; a selection can be saved to a channel and loaded back.

Select and Mask… (Alt+Ctrl+R) refines difficult edges, hair included: seven views (onion skin, on black, on white…), radius, smoothing, feather, colour decontamination, then output to a selection, a mask or a new layer.

Transform

Free Transform (Ctrl+T) sets an eight-handle frame. A corner scales and keeps proportions (Shift frees them), an edge stretches one axis, outside rotates (Shift by 15°), Ctrl distorts or skews. The strip gives X, Y, width, height, angle and interpolation; Enter confirms. Edit › Transform also offers 90° and 180° rotations and flips.

Finally, the Edit menu holds cut, copy, paste (including paste in place), fill and stroke, content-aware fill, Fade to soften the last filter, and the Search palette (Ctrl+K) that finds any command by name.

Adjust and filter

  1. Open Image › Adjustments and pick an adjustment: Levels (Ctrl+L), Curves (Ctrl+M), Hue/Saturation (Ctrl+U), Color Balance, Black & White, Exposure, Vibrance, Shadows/Highlights…
  2. The dialog shows a Preview computed by the engine on a copy. In Curves, a click adds a point, dragging moves it, a double-click removes it, per RGB, red, green or blue channel.
  3. OK applies and adds a history step; Cancel leaves the image untouched.

The Filter menu works the same way: blur (including the blur gallery), sharpen, noise, distort, pixelate, render, stylize, plus the Filter Gallery… and its 47 stackable artistic effects. Last Filter replays with Ctrl+Alt+F. The Image menu also gives automatic corrections, image and canvas size, rotation, and Image › Mode to switch to grayscale, CMYK, Lab, duotone, indexed colour or 16 bits.

Save and send

  1. Save (Ctrl+S) files the image as PNG in the Library, source “Photolab”, with a working file that keeps the layers. It never overwrites: each save is a new file, linked to the original image in its sheet.
  2. Reopened from the Library, a Photolab image comes back with its layers.
  3. Save As… exports to PNG, JPG, WEBP, TIFF, PSD or PCRAFT and downloads it; Save a Copy… leaves the document as is; Export › Quick Export as PNG outputs the image in one click, following your preferences.
  4. Send to… passes the image to another screen, through the Library.

Close (Ctrl+W), Close All and Close Others ask for confirmation on a changed document. Revert (F12) reloads the last save. File Info… holds title, author, keywords and copyright.

Good to know

What the screen cannot do yet opens in the native application: Open in the native app…, then Bring back from the native app returns the document, layers included.

If the engine must restart, the screen says so and returns to the welcome screen: unsaved documents are lost. Save often. The installer checks every engine file; Help › About Photolab gives its version and licences.
