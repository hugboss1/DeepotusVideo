You will give your shots a consistent colour, check them on the scopes, set their speed and framing, add titles and polish the sound. Free: everything is computed on your PC, except subtitle transcription and translation.

Grade a shot

  1. Select a shot: the inspector shows the Grading panel.
  2. Wheels: shadows (lift), midtones (gamma) and highlights (gain). Curves: up to sixteen points, on the whole picture or each colour.
  3. Mask limits the setting to a rectangle or an ellipse, feathered or inverted.
  4. Auto removes a colour cast; Match to previous clip aligns the colours with the shot before.
  5. Copy grade then Paste grade carry the setting over to another shot.

When playback stops, the viewer shows the shot's real graded picture; during playback, the picture stays raw.

Check on the scopes

Scopes opens a movable window: waveform, vectorscope and histogram of the graded picture under the playhead, recomputed when playback stops. They account for speed, stabilisation and the render frame rate, and stay available in full screen.

Effects, speed and framing

The effects rack

Noise reduction, deflicker, debanding, sharpening, green-screen keying, monochrome, per-band hue and saturation, trails, lens distortion.

Speed and ramp

From 25 to 400 %. Interpolation smooths the motion; Split at playhead gives the rest of the shot another speed.

Stabilise and reframe

Stabilisation analyses a shaky shot; framing picks the part kept: centred, following the motion, or manual; dynamic zoom animates a reframe.

Adjustment clip

On track J1 (Shift+J), its effects apply to everything below, with fade in and fade out.

Overlays

Position, scale, rotation and opacity animated by keyframes, rounded corners, drop shadow.

Hand-drawn ramps

An effect ramp can follow a Bézier curve you draw; the shake's ramp raises or lowers its amplitude.

Titles and subtitles

  1. Shift+T places a title card on track T1, burned in at render time.
  2. Pick its template (full frame, lower third, caption, counter, chapter, quote, hashtag, call to action), its text, colour and font.
  3. Subtitles have one track per language: only one is burned in, the others are exported. Translation can create a new language track.

Polish the sound

The audio rack

Six-band equaliser, noise reducer with adjustable floor, 50 / 60 Hz hum removal, stereo pan; listen to the rendered result.

Learn the noise

Set a range on a stretch of noise only: the app learns it and removes it at render time.

Voiceover from the mic

voice-over (Alt+R) records a take while the edit plays and places it on the dialogue track.

Narration

The narration panel (T) writes and synthesises the voice block by block; only “Narrate” sends a block to the provider.

Good to know

In the menu › View, the shot lightbox shows every V1 shot with its graded picture: one click goes to the shot.
