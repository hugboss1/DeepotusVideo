You will be able to film yourself in a 3D set, furnish it with real products, project your images and videos on the set's screen while you talk, and read your script from the prompter. Everything is free except the transcription of a take, on a quote.

Pick and furnish a set

A 3D set adds to the skin: you appear in a studio and the shared screen shows on the set's screen. Eleven sets are built in; each space keeps its own.

  1. In the recording box, Virtual studio group, click Browse sets. Look at a set through its cameras, as a floor plan or in the free 3D view.
  2. A built-in set cannot be edited: Customise this set makes a copy in the space and opens the Decorate panel.
  3. Catalogue tab: Load the catalogue downloads once the list of real products, furniture, lighting, rugs and wall art. Search in French or English, or filter by family.
  4. Pick a product, then Download and place: it arrives at its true size, on the floor, the wall or the ceiling depending on what it is. A lamp lights the set; its colour sets its light.
  5. To change a piece of furniture, select it, click Replace…, then place an item from Objects, Catalogue, Library or Imports: it takes its place and its orientation.
  6. Use this set keeps it for the space: it dresses the next recording.

Prepare the screen's control room

  1. Screen tab: here you prepare the sequence of what the set's screen will show during the take. With no media, it shows the shared screen, as usual.
  2. Add images or videos from the Library. The sequence starts with the shared screen: the screen only changes when you move to the next media.
  3. Reorder the sequence with its arrows. Preview shows a media on the set's screen, in the preview.

Edit live during the take

  1. Start the recording in The app only mode. A control bar appears at the bottom, outside the picture, with a small monitor of what is being recorded.
  2. Talk on the wide shot, then switch to the screen camera: Ctrl+Alt+1 to 6 changes camera.
  3. Ctrl+Alt+Right moves to the next media; a video starts at once, while you keep talking. Ctrl+Alt+Space pauses it.
  4. The buttons of the control bar do the same with the mouse.

Keep your script and read it from the prompter

  1. In the recording box, Script and prompter group, tick "Keep the text of my voice": the browser writes what you say, and that script is stored with the take.
  2. Take scripts… lists your takes: open a script to correct it. A take without a script can be transcribed with Transcribe (paid)…; the price shows before anything is sent.
  3. For the next take, pick that script as the prompter text, or paste another one.
  4. During the take, the prompter scrolls under the REC pill, outside the picture. Its bar sets pause, speed and text size.

Good to know

The control bar and the prompter only show in The app only mode: in The whole tab, they would be recorded. The sound of a projected video is recorded only if Add the app's sound is ticked; otherwise it stays silent.

Browser recognition sends your voice through its service: it only listens if you tick the box. A video in the control room is not copied: if it leaves the Library, the set's screen stays black.

The catalogue is free and needs an Internet connection. Its products are under the CC BY 4.0 licence: crediting Amazon.com is required. The Imports tab prepares the set credits, ready to paste under the video.
