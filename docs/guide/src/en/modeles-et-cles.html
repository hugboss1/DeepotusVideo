You will know how to read a list of AI models: which key is used, what one run costs, and what to do when a key is missing. Five minutes, free; the same lists are used across the whole app.

Two routes for the same model

Wherever you generate (images, videos, sounds, background removal), the model list is sorted into groups, one per route. You keep all your keys: each list offers every route, and you choose model by model.

With the fal key

A single fal.ai key opens most models: FLUX, Nano Banana, GPT Image, Seedream, Veo, Kling… The label says "via fal" when the same model also exists direct.

With your key

The model paid directly to its provider: OpenAI, Gemini, ElevenLabs, Meshy, HeyGen. Handy if you already have an account and credits there.

Local, free

Some tools run on your PC, such as rembg background removal or plain background: no key, no spending.

Read an option's price

Each option shows its unit price after the name: $0.04/image, $0.10/s of video, $0.0018/s of sound, /30 images for a video cutout. An option marked price to be measured is paid, but its rate isn't known yet: it is never shown as free.

Before a run, the screen shows the total price worked out by the server for what you ask: an image and its background removal, a 6 s video… Your spending caps check this very amount, see Keep spending under control.

Text engines

The text engine lists (the Vectorlab AI dialog, the roles of Settings › Provider defaults) gather three kinds of engines. The tooltip of the Vectorlab Generate button says, for the chosen engine, what the call costs.

Built-in

Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini: paid on your key, a few hundredths of a cent per illustration or summary.

Free providers

The fl:… engines (DeepSeek, OpenRouter…): price to be measured. The cap applies to a per-token estimate, at the dearest built-in rate.

Local servers

ollama and the local:… engines run on your PC or your network: free, nothing is charged.

An "add the key" option

  1. An option whose key is missing stays visible and can be chosen; it carries the note "add the … key in Settings".
  2. If you run it, the run is refused at once, before any spending, naming the missing key.
  3. Add the key in Settings › API keys (see Connect your keys): it works right away, no restart. Or pick the same option with the fal key.

Good to know

Model names (Flux 3, Veo 3.1 Fast, Sonilo…) are proper names: they stay the same in French and English.

A run whose price exceeds your cap is refused before the call. The most expensive actions run in two clicks: the first shows the estimate, the second runs it.
