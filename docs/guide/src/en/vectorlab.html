You will be able to draw a poster, a logo or a sheet in vectors in Vectorlab: shapes, pen, text, appearance, layers, symbols, then send it to other screens. Free, everything is computed on your PC; only AI illustration is paid, price shown first.

Open Vectorlab

  1. Open Vectorlab in the app bar. The home lists your documents: one click on a card opens it. Pick a format (square, 16:9, A4, playing card…) then Create for a new document.
  2. A document also comes from elsewhere: Send to… › Vectorlab from the Library or Photolab, “Edit this face in Vectorlab” from Card Forge, “Open in Vectorlab” from a Templates layout.
  3. At the top left, two pills: Vector to draw, Pixel to retouch pixels (see the next chapter). Each has its own tools and panels.

The screen follows Affinity's conventions: ten menus at the top (File, Edit, Document, Text, Vector, Pixel, Layer, View, Window, Help), a context bar showing the selection and the current tool's settings, tools on the left, the tabbed panel stack on the right and a status bar that recalls the tool's gestures. The document tab shows “name @ zoom” and a star when it is not saved.

Pick a tool

Tools are grouped in families: only the current tool of each family is visible. The corner triangle or a long press opens the family, right-click opens the tool's settings, the letter picks it from the keyboard.

Select (V)

The marquee only takes objects fully inside (Alt: those it touches). Shift constrains, Ctrl resizes from the centre, Alt+drag copies, Ctrl+click reaches an object inside a group, Tab goes to the next one.

Pen (P) and Node (N)

Click = sharp node, drag = smooth node, click the first node to close. Four modes: Pen, Smart, Polygon, Line. The Node tool pulls handles, bends a segment, adds (double-click) or deletes nodes.

Shapes (R, E, L, F)

Rectangle, ellipse, line and parametric shapes (polygon, star, cog, arrow, donut, spiral) adjustable by handles. A single click places a shape; the Corner tool (C) rounds a corner.

Freehand

Smoothed pencil (B), vector brush (J) with flat, tapered or calligraphic profile, eraser (W), knife (X) and shape builder (S) that combines or removes areas by clicks.

Text (T)

Drag = text sized to the height you pull; click on a curve = text on that path; double-click to edit in place. The text frame flows text and flags overflow in red.

Eyedropper, measure, view

Eyedropper (I) with loupe (Ctrl: the whole style), measure (M), hand (H), zoom (Z, Alt to zoom out). Ctrl+0 fits the page, Ctrl+1 shows 100%.

Set the appearance

  1. Select an object. The context bar gives X, Y, W, H and opacity; fields accept calculations (100/3).
  2. The Appearance tab sets fill and stroke from the swatches, no fill or no stroke, width, opacity. Stroke and Transform have their own tabs (skew, movable pivot).
  3. Appearance + goes further: linear, radial and conical gradients, transparency, patterns (hatching, dots, checkerboard), multiple strokes, six effects, 16 blend modes, global colours, harmonies, object styles, paste inside a shape (clipping mask), text in a frame or on a path.

An offset stroke and a text on a path stay live: they recompute when their source changes, until you detach them.

Layers and symbols

The Layers tab shows the layer's opacity and blend mode at the top, then each layer with its thumbnail, lock and eye; a triangle unfolds it into a tree of its objects, clips, masks and effects. The Layer menu creates, renames, groups (Ctrl+G), locks, hides and orders.

A symbol is created with Vector › Create Symbol; its instances follow every change. Double-click an instance to edit it in place, then Done or Discard.

The Vector menu

Geometry

Add, subtract, intersect and divide shapes; join, reverse, break at node; Round corners.

Convert to curves

A text becomes paths (Ctrl+Enter), as one or one path per glyph.

Image trace

Image Trace… vectorises a placed image (colours, smoothing, threshold), with preview, on your PC.

The document

The Document menu holds Setup… (size, resolution, unit, background), Artboards… exported separately, Margins and bleed… (guides never exported), the Document grid… (square, iso, triangular, hexagonal, snapping), Board and terrains… to paint tiles (K) and the Real map…. View › Snap to objects snaps to edges, centres and even gaps.

File › Place image… or Place from Library… places an image; the Stock tab shows the Library with search, one click places the image. An image can be cropped, locked and also receives effects.

Good to know

The document is saved as a draft every 30 seconds: when you reopen it, a window offers to restore it or to start from the server. History keeps 1000 steps; Edit › Add Snapshot marks a state to come back to.

Hover an option for a second, or click the “?” next to it, for its animated card. F1 lists every shortcut; Edit › Settings (Ctrl+,) sets tooltips, grid and snapping.

AI illustration (AI family, or File › New image processing) is paid: the price is shown first, within your caps. Everything else is free.
