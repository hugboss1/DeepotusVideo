You will make a vertical video in minutes: an animated clip from a picture, an avatar saying your text, both together, or a voiceover. Paid depending on the provider; the cost is shown before you click.

Before you start

Quick has four tabs: Seedance (animated clip), HeyGen (talking avatar), Composition (avatar and clip together) and Voiceover. A tab stays greyed out while its provider's key is missing: fal.ai for Seedance, HeyGen for the avatar (chapter Connect your keys). The voiceover can also run for free on your PC with Voicebox.

Animate a picture into a clip

  1. Open Quick, Seedance tab.
  2. Pick the start image: drop it on the area or click Browse thumbnails… to take it from the Library. An end image is optional; if the chosen model does not accept one, the field says so and names the models that do.
  3. Write what should move, or start from a prompt template, or click Open prompt generator.
  4. In Parameters, set the model, the duration and the format (9:16 by default).
  5. Read the estimated cost, then click the Generate button. The render goes through the render queue, top right, and lands in the Library.

With the default model, Seedance 2.5 at 720p, one second costs about $0.47: a 10 s clip comes to about $4.73. A shorter duration or another model costs less; the estimated cost updates with every setting.

Direct the camera

  1. Unfold Camera (sliders): six sliders (push in, turn, rise…) become a sentence suited to the model, added to the prompt. It is shown before the render.
  2. To choose by eye, click Gallery: eleven camera moves in three styles, as small animations computed on your PC. It is a reminder, not a promise of what the model will do.

Make an avatar talk

  1. HeyGen tab: pick an avatar and its voice. The lists show up right away, from a copy kept on your PC and refreshed in the background.
  2. For an avatar that looks like you, import a photo: it becomes a talking avatar, selected automatically.
  3. Write the script, then generate. The pauses in your text are kept.

Videos go through HeyGen and its Avatar III engine, picked according to the avatar. The HeyGen balance is shown in dollars. Count about $0.24 a minute at default rates.

Compose and dub

Composition

An avatar and a clip in the same video, one after the other or side by side (vertical or horizontal split).

Voiceover

Choose the provider for each generation: Voicebox, on your PC and free, or ElevenLabs. A missing provider is refused, never silently replaced.

Music under the voice

When a render has both a voice and music, the music dips on its own under the voice.

Burned-in subtitles

Subtitle the render: aligning a text you provide is free; automatic transcription is paid and only runs if you tick it.

Go further

Presets

At the top of each tab, save your settings under a name and reapply them in one click.

Lip-sync

Syncs the clip's lips to a voiceover (Kling, paid); limits and price are shown beforehand.

Reopen in Quick

Every render keeps its settings: Reopen in Quick, from the Library or the render queue, puts them back as they were.

Extend a clip

From Send to, Extend adds about 7 generated seconds to a clip already rendered, with or without sound; the price is on each button.

Good to know

Leaving Quick and coming back finds your settings as you left them.

For a still picture, use the Library: Create image, with the Prompt manager. For your own filmed video, Upload video in the Library, then the UGC video node of the Studio.
