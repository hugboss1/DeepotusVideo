You will turn a text, a chapter or a novel into a narrated, illustrated video, scene by scene, and prepare its storyboard before paying for any render. Free up to narration and pictures; their cost is shown beforehand.

Two ways of working

Open Chapters in the rail. The original flow (Episodes) goes straight from text to narrated video in four steps. The Chapter Workshop is the full space: manuscript, bible of characters and places, screenplay, storyboard, animatic.

An episode in four steps

  1. Script and voice: give a title, paste the text or upload a .txt, .docx or .pdf. Pick the voice and language, then Generate narration: the whole chapter is read and stored in the audio Library.
  2. Scenes: split the text by paragraph (word for word) or by AI (into visual beats, with an illustration prompt). Edit, reorder, add or remove scenes.
  3. Illustrations: one picture per scene, with the model of your choice. For each scene, pick the animation: slow camera move (Ken Burns), real Seedance clip or still picture.
  4. Assembly and export: a quote gives the price of the Seedance scenes, then the episode is cut on its narration. If a Seedance scene fails, it falls back to a camera move and you are told.

At the top, New, Open and Save manage your episodes; a dot marks what is not saved. Narrate scenes never pays again for a scene already narrated with the same text, voice and language.

The Chapter Workshop

The Workshop is not translated yet: its buttons below are quoted as they appear, in French.

  1. Script: write or import the chapter text; Manuscrit imports a whole novel, split into chapters, and fills the bible. Recognised names are highlighted.
  2. Bible, on the right: characters, places, objects, dates, moods, sets. Each entry has its description, its picture sheet and, for a character, its voice and temperament.
  3. Screenplay: Adapter (IA) (adapt with AI) rewrites the chapter as a screenplay without touching the manuscript; Voice-over has each scene read by its character's voice.
  4. Storyboard: Découper (IA) (split with AI) or Paragraphes (paragraphs) creates the shots, with their framing, camera, duration and the entities in them.
  5. Animatique (animatic) cuts the shots into a rehearsal video, silent and free, to watch before paying for a render. It then goes into a new Edit project, as a full film or a 30 s reel.

Go further

Versions

Every time the text is overwritten, a copy is kept (ten at most): compare, restore, copy.

Exports

The manuscript as .docx or PDF, the screenplay in industry format, the storyboard as a PDF with four shots per page.

Import a screenplay

A Fountain or Final Draft file replaces the scenes (the old screenplay is kept) or is added after them.

Production picture

A shot's picture is generated with the characters' views as references; the cost is stated and confirmed.

Appearances

A bible entry lists its mentions, shots and scenes; one click opens the shot in the storyboard.

To an episode

Épisode (episode) creates a narrated episode (one shot, one scene) without paying anything; narration and render then start in Episodes.

Good to know

Rewriting a selection in the bible's tone, generating a production picture or adding the animatic's guide voice is paid: the cost is always stated and confirmed first.
