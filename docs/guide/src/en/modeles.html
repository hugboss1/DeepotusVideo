You will start from a ready-made layout, touch it up (slots, masks, texts, animations), adapt it to another format and dress it in your brand. Free; only renders of AI-generated slots cost money.

Pick a template

  1. Open Templates in the rail. The gallery shows a real picture of each layout; the search field filters by name or tag.
  2. Click a card to open it in the editor, or create a blank one with New and a format (9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5).
  3. For the same layout in another format, Reflow to creates a copy with a before / after preview; the original does not change.

Touch up a layout

  1. Click a slot on the canvas: drag its body to move it, its handles to resize it. The inspector, on the right, shows its settings.
  2. Mask gives a picture or video slot a shape: rounded, ellipse, polygon (triangle, diamond, hexagon, star, or your own points), cut-out windows, soft edge, coloured rim.
  3. Animation brings the slot in and out with a fade, slide or pop, with duration, delay and curve. Replay on canvas shows it.
  4. Add places a text, a ticker, a separator, the brand, a badge or a component.
  5. Save layout, then Open in Studio to fill it in and render it.

Polish the texts

Ready-made styles

Social subtitle, neon, banner, gradient title; or your own settings: outline, shadow, rounded background, gradient.

Text that fits

The text stays in its slot: it wraps, then shrinks down to a minimum size.

Text on arc

Sets a title as an arch or a smile; an arc too large is reduced to fit.

Exact preview

Renders the slot with the real render engine, to see the final result.

Dress it with your brand

Templates use your brand kit: name, colours, logo. Kits are managed in Settings › Branding (chapter Settings, personas and branding). A render keeps the kit active when it was sent, even if you switch while it computes.

Go further

Components

Save as component turns slots into a reusable group; each instance can change its texts and colours, then go back to the component.

Preview sample

A Library picture fills a slot for thumbnails and picture export; never for the video render.

Export

Export image as PNG, JPEG or WebP (it lands in the Library), or as an SVG to drop into Figma.

To the Vectorlab

Open in the Vectorlab opens the layout as an editable vector document.

From Figma

Import a Figma frame… turns it into an editable template; the number of Figma calls is announced beforehand (Figma key required).

Avatar in the right format

A HeyGen avatar is generated in the format closest to its slot: no empty band, no cut-off head.

Good to know

In the Studio, the layout node shows the transition planned by the template as long as you do not pick another. The “cyan flash” transition is a real colour flash, of your choice.
