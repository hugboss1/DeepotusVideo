One icon, one function

The whole app speaks the same language: the Deepotus Glyph set, 526 icons drawn with the same stroke. Each one stands for a single function: the same drawing means the same thing in the editor, the Photolab or the Card Forge. The old emojis and symbols are gone from buttons, menus and lists.

  1. Rest the pointer on a button: its tooltip gives its name and, if it has one, its keyboard shortcut.
  2. Look that name up in the icon sheet to read what it does and what not to confuse it with.
  3. In the Vectorlab, the Photolab, the Sprite Lab and the Tile Lab, wait a little longer (900 ms) or click the small ? button: an animated card SHOWS you the option.

Icon families

Icons are sorted into families. Knowing the family already tells you what to expect when you click.

Navigation

A screen, a tab or a panel: you go there, nothing is changed.

Actions

A button that does something: create, save, send, delete.

Editing

Change something already there: align, flip, group, lock.

Tools

The drawing and retouching tools of the labs.

Layers and adjustments

Layers, masks, styles and image adjustments.

Media and playback

Play, cut and edit video and sound.

States

What the app tells you, without you having to click.

Categories

Content families, each with its own colour.

3D

Objects and actions of the 3D workshops.

States worth knowing

A state icon is not clicked: it informs you. These are the ones to read before starting a paid render.

IconWhat it says
Done: the render, the upload or the save worked.
In progress or waiting: loading, generating, or queued.
Failed: the tooltip or the message next to it says why.
Warning: nothing is broken, but read before going on.
Spending limit close or reached: beyond it, the app refuses the spend and asks you what to do.
Generative field: AI can fill it in for you.
Locked: nothing can move, change or delete the item while it is.
Pinned (Studio): this node result is reused for free on the next render.

Print the icon sheet

The icon sheet shows them all, sorted by family, with their name, their function and the look-alike icons not to confuse them with.

  1. Open the icon sheet.
  2. To print only part of it, click a family (for example Vectorlab tools) or type a word in the search: only what is shown gets printed.
  3. Click Print: each family starts on a new A4 page, black on white. You can also download the full sheet as a PDF.

On the sheet, the red line starting with “≠” names the look-alike icons: Close hides a window, Delete destroys, Remove takes something out of a list without destroying it.

Good to know

The icon of the app, of its shortcuts and of browser tabs is the real Deepotus logo. Inside the app, the logo shown follows your active brand kit (chapter Templates and brand kits).

One single chevron is used everywhere to expand and collapse: pointing down, the section is open; pointing right, it is closed.
