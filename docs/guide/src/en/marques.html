You will run several brands in the same installation (Deepotus, a new brand, a client) without ever mixing them up: each has its own persona, identity, Quick templates, publishing accounts, Library and spending. Free; only the assistant's AI proposals are paid, and their price is shown first.

Create a brand's space

  1. Open Settings › Brand spaces. Your installation already has one space: Deepotus, which keeps everything you had before.
  2. Type the brand's name, pick its colour, then Create space. The new space is empty and not active yet.
  3. On its row, Define the persona opens an eight-step assistant: what you promote, the audience, the character, tone and voice, the visual world, references, what is off-limits, then the synthesis. Nothing is written until you validate.
  4. Persona identity stores the logo, the mascot, product photos, the palette, fonts and a written style. The prompt engine uses it instead of the Deepotus world.
  5. Click Activate: the whole app switches to this space.

Switch from one brand to another

Two places, one setting: the Active space menu under the brand card at the bottom of the rail, and the persona menu at the top of Quick. Changing one changes the other, and the Library too. In Quick, the prompt templates, the vibes and the default voice-over become those of the chosen brand.

  2. The Prompt templates reload with the space's templates. A new space has none yet: Space templates proposes some from the persona, without AI and for free; tick the ones to keep.
  3. + New persona…, at the bottom of the same menu, opens Settings › Brand spaces to create one.

Publish on the brand's accounts

Accounts per space

Under the list of spaces, Publishing accounts manages the active space's accounts: several X or Telegram accounts if needed, each with its own credentials. Test checks an account; Connect starts the YouTube or TikTok consent.

The Scheduler follows the space

It shows the active space's posts. In a post, Publishing accounts only offers this brand's accounts.

Never on the wrong account

A client's post cannot go out on a Deepotus account: the app refuses to send it.

Sort files and bill

Library per space

The header menu shows This space or All spaces. Each render is filed in the space where it was started, even if you switch spaces while it runs.

Spending and cap

Each space's row shows what it cost this month. A monthly cap of its own comes on top of the global cap; 0 means no cap.

Statement for the client

Month statement (CSV) downloads the space's spending, ready to attach to an invoice.

Hand over or archive a client's space

  1. Export, on the space's row, downloads a .zip package: persona, identity and its pictures, Quick templates, brand kit and the list of accounts. No secret goes in: tokens and passwords stay on your PC.
  2. On another installation, Import a space… creates a new space from the package, never replacing one. The message says how many accounts are waiting for their credentials: enter them again in Publishing accounts.
  3. When a contract ends, Archive puts the space away: it leaves the menus, and its scheduled posts are paused. Unarchive, with Show archived spaces, resumes them.

Good to know

Only one space is active at a time, for the whole app. A render, a post or a task already started keeps its space until the end.

The app's logo and name never change with the space: they are set in Settings › Branding. The kit chosen for a space is only used for that brand's content.

The active space cannot be archived: activate another one first. There is always at least one space.
