You will be able to block a shot in 3D on the Stage before any paid run: place volumes or a real mesh, frame at the film's format, set a camera move, then send it all back to the storyboard shot. Free and local.

Compose the scene

  1. From a shot card of the Workshop storyboard, the Stage button opens that shot's scene; Workshop brings you back.
  2. Place simple volumes: Box, Capsule, Cylinder, Sphere, or a mesh from a 3D job or a bible entity, chosen in the list (grouped under “Bible entities” and “3D meshes”) then placed with the button next to it.
  3. Each object has a name, a role (subject, set dressing, marker), a position, a rotation, a scale and its measured dimensions; Duplicate or Remove.

Frame and animate

Frame

16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 2.39:1 format, focal length in millimetres, guides (thirds, crosses, title safe, horizon). The shot type (wide, medium, close-up) is measured from the subject's height in the frame.

Motion

K places a camera keyframe at the playhead; a preset lays a dolly-in, a 90° orbit, a crane down or a locked-off shot. Space plays the move in a loop.

Outputs

  1. Capture start / end: the frame of the first and last keyframe goes to the Library, with its provenance.
  2. Compose the GLB: the scene's GLB, versioned with its sheet.
  3. Apply to shot…: proposes to the linked shot the shot type, the move, the motion prompt and the end images, compared before and after; nothing changes without your confirmation.

Good to know

The Stage is for deciding before paying: the captured end images become the start and end frames of a video shot, and the measured move feeds its prompt.
