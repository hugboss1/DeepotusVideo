You will fill your week with posts, review them, approve them in one go and let them go out on time on Telegram, X, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. The Scheduler is free; only what you produce (image, clip, avatar) has a price, shown before the run.

Before you start

For a post to go out on its own, its network must be connected: see Connect your accounts. Without a connected account everything still works in assisted mode: the post is ready at the set time and you click Send.

The week at a glance

Open Scheduler in the rail. At the top, the calendar in Week or Month view. Click a post: its graph (render, caption, one node per channel) shows in the middle and its inspector on the right. The screen fits the window from 1280 to 1600 px; the toolbar wraps when needed.

Fill the week

  1. Generate plan: describe the week in one sentence, pick the number of days, posts per day, language, start date and channels, then Generate. Without an AI key the built-in planner does the job; with an Anthropic key or a local Ollama model the plan is finer.
  2. Review the proposal, then Add to calendar. Times follow your per-channel time slots.
  3. Already have a written schedule? The Import doc tab turns a .md, .txt, .docx or .pdf file into dated posts, keeping weeks and themes.
  4. For a single post: New post, or add post at the bottom of a day. Episodes from Chapters land here as drafts.

Prepare a post

In the inspector, set the day and time, tick Scheduled (unticked, the post stays a draft), pick the channels and write the caption, the same on every channel, with its character count. Produce with chosen source makes the visual or clip the plan calls for; the render runs in the background and its thumbnail appears on the graph's Render node.

The final preview shows the post as it will look on the network. For a Reel, a Short or a TikTok, it darkens the top and bottom bands and the right rail that the network's interface covers: put nothing important there.

Approve and publish

  1. Each post has a mode. Assisted (default): at the set time it flips to ready and you click Send. Auto (Auto-publish at time): it goes out on its own on its connected channels.
  2. Approve the week then Approve the 7 days switches the drafts and scheduled posts of the next 7 days to automatic in one go. A post without media is skipped and named; really editing an approved post puts it back on hold.
  3. To publish right now: Send now on the graph, or the yellow inspector button that counts the channels.

Before each send, the network's quota is checked (X 500 posts a month, Instagram 100 per rolling 24 h, YouTube 100 a day, TikTok 15 a day); a channel already published is never published again.

Going further

Time slots

Time slots: local times per channel, separated by commas, then Save. Suggest from my metrics picks the hour with the best engagement, from 5 measured posts.

Analytics

Views, likes and engagement over the last 28 days by channel, format and week, with the top 5. Metrics are re-read every day; Refresh now re-reads 10 posts.

Campaign brief

Campaign › Campaign brief: objective, key messages, fixed sections and banned terms. The next plan follows it; banned terms are removed from captions and hashtags.

Recurring series

A rule (days, time, channels, caption template with {date}, {weekday}, {week}): Create series, then Create 4 weeks. These are drafts; creating twice duplicates nothing.

Suggested recycling

Your best-measured posts, published more than three weeks ago, come back with a varied caption. Suggest for tomorrow creates a draft; no paid AI is called.

X thread

Follow-up (X thread) writes the follow-up to an X post: it goes out two minutes later and replies to the previous message, never before it.

Duplicate, sign

Duplicate to next day copies the post. Under the caption, a row of emojis and the Telegram Premium pack insert your brand tags, set in Settings.

With the phone

Approved posts form a batch the paired phone can take along. A post handed to the phone says so in the inspector, with "Take back on the PC".

Good to know

After an action (approve, create a series, suggest a recycle, add a follow-up), the list reloads by itself: no need to leave the screen.

The calendar, built-in plans, approval and sending are free. Producing an image, a clip or an avatar is paid, under your spending caps (see Control your spending).
