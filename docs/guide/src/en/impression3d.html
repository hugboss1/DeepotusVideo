You will be able to send everything the app produces to your 3D printer: generated models, Workbench parts, 3D Forge cards and Vectorlab drawings. Everything is local and free: no provider call, and the app never generates G-code.

Before you start

The pipeline is designed for the Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2 (256 × 256 × 256 mm bed) and its slicer ElegooSlicer, shipped with the machine; OrcaSlicer works too. Install the slicer once: Windows associates .3mf files with it, and Open in the slicer uses that. Without an association, the app tells you; then set SLICER_PATH (the slicer's path) in the data folder's .env file.

Each export writes a folder in DeepotusVideoGenData\assets\print3d\: the STL (the mesh), the 3MF (the one you open, in millimetres and named) and impression.json (provenance, scale, watertightness).

Where to export from

  1. From the Workbench: Export tab, 3D printing writes the displayed version (repaired, laid down, hollowed…) at the target size. It is the recommended path: see Prepare a part.
  2. From Game Assets · 3D: each generated model has its 3D printing button. Give the largest dimension in millimetres (80 for a figurine); the part is centred and placed on the floor. The file “optimised” for game engines is refused with a message saying what to take.
  3. From the cards' 3D Forge: when every element is a closed solid, the manifest delivers an STL and the print button appears. Leave the size empty to keep the card's real millimetres. Watertightness is marked guaranteed there.
  4. From Vectorlab: File › 3D Print… turns the drawing into parts: one height per layer, tiles, a bevelled logo, a map relief or pixel art. See 3D printing from Vectorlab.
  5. Slice in the slicer. The 3MF opens at the right size, laid on the bed. Supports, fine orientation, infill, filament profiles: that is the slicer's job. Above 256 mm, the export warns you without blocking you.

Good to know

The watertightness of meshes from image-to-3D engines is marked unknown in impression.json: ElegooSlicer and OrcaSlicer repair small defects on import and report it. Go through the Workbench first to repair in one click. Only the cards' 3D Forge guarantees its solids, through its “closed solid” check.
