## Overview

PixVerse v6 animates a start image. It does not accept an end image. It has a seed, a negative field and a resolution
choice. The frame follows the start image: it has no format field. It has few durations: read them in Members.

## Quick reference

- [CHOICE] Use it for a short shot where only the opening matters. For a shot that must end on a precise image, use
  a member that accepts an end image.
- [METHOD] The seed lets you compare: keep the same image, seed and duration, then change a single clause of the
  prompt per run. The difference then comes from that clause.
- [PITFALL] The seed does not guarantee the same take from one resolution to another. A low-resolution draft
  validates the motion, not the final clip.
- [PROMPT] One action and one named camera move per shot, in sentences. Describe only the motion: the image already
  carries the subject and the setting.
- [PROMPT] Negative: a short list of visible artifacts ("flicker, warped hands, on-screen text"). A long list
  stiffens the motion.
- [PITFALL] The format follows the image: a landscape image gives a landscape clip. Prepare the start image in the
  wanted format before the run.
- [PITFALL] Native durations are fixed. A longer target duration is extended by the app (ffmpeg), not generated by
  the model: for more motion, chain two shots from the last frame.
- [COST] Find the motion at low resolution, at the final duration, then render once at high resolution.
- [CHOICE] Not in the app yet: the provider's style presets and effects. Do not promise them.

## Example

Brief: "A vertical Instagram story: the perfume bottle on a wet rock, a wave laps at the rock, the camera circles a
little." The 9:16 start image is approved.

Prompt:

```
A small wave rolls in and washes over the base of the wet rock, then pulls back. The camera makes a slow orbit to
the right around the perfume bottle. Soft overcast light, gentle sea spray in the air.
```

Settings: PixVerse v6; short duration; low resolution for two or three tries at a fixed seed, then high resolution
for the chosen clip; negative: "flicker, distorted bottle, on-screen text"; end image: not available.

## Common mistakes

- Picking it for a shot that must land on a given image: it has no end image. Switch family.
- Changing the prompt, seed and resolution at once: you no longer know what improved the clip.
- Believing the seed will reproduce at high resolution the take seen at low resolution.
- Starting from a landscape image for a vertical story: the clip keeps the image's format.
